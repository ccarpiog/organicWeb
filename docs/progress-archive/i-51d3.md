## What was built

- **The acid checks** (`acidChecks()`, first in `firstRule()`, when the engine's principal group is a –COOH; each rule 1,
  step `group`, «Reconoce el grupo funcional», which says the three atoms are one group, how the –COOH is written and,
  beside other oxygen groups, the seniority):
  - **ACID_GROUP** (new) — any –COOH of the molecule (`moleculeCarboxyls()`, since the review fix) whose carbon is no
    acid suffix's and no `carboxi`'s of the student's (`studentCarboxylCarbons()`): its C=O and OH written as two groups
    (`1-hidroxipropanal`, `1-oxopropan-1-ol`, `1-hidroxi-1-oxopropano`, `(hidroxioxometil)ciclohexano`, `ácido
    4-hidroxi-4-oxobutanoico`; outside the engine's suffix `1-hidroxi-2-(4-carboxiciclohexil)etanal`). Focus: the carbon,
    both oxygens and their bonds; tiles: the student's prefix holding one of the oxygens, else the suffix.
  - **CARBONYL_SENIORITY** (extended, `group` 'acid') — a junior suffix beside the –COOH; the junior's prefix from
    `juniorPrefix()` (`oxo`, `hidroxi`, and for an aldehyde `oxo` on the parent or `formil` hanging from it).
  - **KETONE_NOT_ALDEHYDE** (reused) — an `al` beside the –COOH on a carbon a branch bonds to a second carbon
    (`1-carboxibutanal`): CARBONYL_SENIORITY steps aside rather than speak of «el –CHO del aldehído».
  - **ACID_CARBON** (new, ALDEHYDE_CARBON's twin through `groupCarbon()`) — `carboxílico` on a chain, or the –COOH's
    carbon as the student's chain beside a ring (templates included), only for the engine's own –COOH groups.
  - **CARBONYL_PREFIX** (extended) — a parent-level `carboxi` whose own C=O is one of the engine's suffix oxygens while
    the student's suffix cites fewer –COOH groups; «Su carbono entra en la cadena principal» on a chain.
- **The rules with the –COOH's words** (`group` 'acid'): CARBONYL_PARENT (ring or chain, `RINGCHAIN` counts),
  MORE_CARBONYL_GROUPS (P0, also before BRANCH_AT_END), ETHER_SIDE told from either, NUMBERING / RING_NUMBERING /
  CHAIN_LOCANTS at N0 (`carbonylN0.acid`), NEEDLESS_LOCANTS (`suffixWhat()`), MISSING_LOCANTS for a suffix locant.
- **How the –COOH is written** (`DIAGNOSIS_PHRASES.ending()`, `principalEnding()`): «con la palabra «ácido» al principio
  del nombre y la terminación «-oico» al final» (`-dioico`, `-carboxílico`, `-tricarboxílico`…), or «con «ácido
  benzoico» (el benceno con un grupo –COOH)».
- **Focus:** a student's –COOH suffix group now includes its OH (`suffixFocus()`).

## Decisions

- **Codes: the C=O groups' where the message holds, new where it must differ.** The aldehyde/ketone codes whose
  template already takes a group (CARBONYL_SENIORITY, CARBONYL_PREFIX, CARBONYL_PARENT, MORE_CARBONYL_GROUPS, the N0
  phrases) are extended with `group` 'acid' — the –COOH is a principal group with a C=O of its own, and the student reads
  only the message, which names the –COOH. Two new codes: ACID_GROUP (no carbonyl message says that a C=O and an OH on one
  carbon are one group) and ACID_CARBON (ALDEHYDE_CARBON is named after the aldehyde, as HYDROXY_PARENT was after the OH
  when CARBONYL_PARENT was added).
- **Order** `ACID_GROUP → CARBONYL_SENIORITY → KETONE_NOT_ALDEHYDE → ACID_CARBON → CARBONYL_PREFIX → principalParent →
  rules`: a –COOH split in two is the first thing the group step explains; seniority before the prefix check so
  `4-carboxibutan-2-ona` explains why the acid wins; DIAGNOSES puts ACID_GROUP first (acidChecks() runs first) and
  ACID_CARBON beside ALDEHYDE_CARBON.
- **KETONE_NOT_ALDEHYDE before the seniority for a ketone's `al`.** In `1-carboxibutanal` the student's `al` is no
  aldehyde (its carbon is bonded to two carbons): CARBONYL_SENIORITY would have said «el –CHO del aldehído va delante,
  con el prefijo «oxo»», which is false; KETONE_NOT_ALDEHYDE says what that C=O is and quotes the acid's name.
- **Wording «grupo –COOH»**, as the explanation's own steps (explain.js SUFFIX_GROUP_WORDS), and «ácido > aldehído >
  cetona > alcohol» as its acid group step says it; «la palabra «ácido» al principio del nombre», as the step's «el
  nombre empieza por la palabra «ácido»».
- **CHAIN_LOCANTS and NUMBERING at N0 are worded but unreachable for an acid:** a –COOH's carbon is bonded to one carbon,
  so it ends any chain that holds it (locant 1 or the last), chains as long with as many –COOH groups tie at N0, and
  `oico` puts the student's at 1; `carboxílico` on a chain is ACID_CARBON first. No sweep or random name reached them.
- **MISSING_LOCANTS for a suffix locant** names the suffix's groups («…y cada grupo –COOH»), for every family (no
  test reached it with a suffix locant before; every earlier message is unchanged). On an acid it is said only by
  diagnose() on a `missingLocants` verdict: no later rule meets a missing `carboxílico` locant (letters are refused
  beside it, old-style locants with it are refused, SEGMENT_ORDER with it gives `ambiguous`).
- **The one null left** (BEYOND): a student's acid suffix while the engine's principal group is no –COOH. It cannot be
  built (a student's `oico`/`carboxílico` builds a –COOH, the most senior group the app names; an ester's principal group
  is outside the rules), so it is tested with a made-up naming.
- **Every –COOH of the molecule is checked for a split, not only the engine's suffix groups** (review fix): a carbon with
  a lone double-bonded O and a lone OH is a –COOH wherever it is; if no acid suffix group and no `carboxi` (at any depth)
  of the student's has that carbon, the student wrote it as two groups, and ACID_GROUP speaks before any seniority,
  ketone or alcohol message. When none of the split groups is one of the engine's suffix groups (`principal` false),
  ACID_GROUP's second sentence says what is true of it: «Ese –COOH no da la terminación del nombre: se nombra con el
  prefijo «carboxi», delante. La terminación es para el otro grupo –COOH, que se nombra …» (plural «los otros grupos
  –COOH, que se nombran todos juntos …»). `hidroxi` + `formil` on one carbon cannot be written (a `formil` takes no
  prefixes); every other split form (`al` / `ol` / `oxo` / `hidroxi`, at the parent or in a branch) is covered.
