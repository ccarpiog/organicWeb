# I-28 — v2.8 Benzene and hydrocarbon derivatives

- Spec: design §13.4 row I-28; `autoclaude/processed/215-rings-functional-groups-confirmed.md`
  §3 item 8.
- Risk: high. Worker: opus (implementation), opus (review fixes).

## Acceptance

- New pure `src/naming/aromatic.js`: benzene = a 6-carbon ring whose ring bonds alternate,
  three ring double bonds, each ring atom in exactly one. Names `benceno`, `metilbenceno`,
  `etenilbenceno`, `isopropilbenceno` (alternatives `(propan-2-il)benceno`,
  `(1-metiletil)benceno`) and so on, no locant — met (`tests/unit/benzene.test.js`, fixtures in
  `tests/fixtures/names.tsv`).
- Both Kekulé forms give identical results, whatever the atom ids, drawing order or
  coordinates; cyclobutadiene, cyclooctatetraene and the cyclohexadienes are still named as
  cycloalkenes — met (invariance tests plus negative fixtures).
- Two or more substituents → `CYCLE`, `ringReason: 'polysubstitutedBenzene'`, message "Este
  benceno tiene N sustituyentes…"; a substituent containing a ring stays `RING_SYSTEM` — met.
- `fenil`/`phenyl` retained prefix in both lexicons, unit-tested; no hydrocarbon name uses it
  (ring senior to chain, design §13.5) — met.
- Explanation step "Reconoce el benceno": the two Kekulé drawings are one molecule, one
  substituent needs no number, the ring beats the chain, and «feniletano» is not preferred —
  met (snapshots updated on purpose).
- Editor: "Benceno" button in Anillos; `a` cycles 3…8 → benceno; placed free, hung from an
  atom or fused on a bond; one undo step per gesture — met (unit + e2e).
- Ordenar dibujo orders benzenes with the ring strategy; the 90° view still falls back — met.
- OPSIN adapter turns aromatic SMILES into Kekulé form and accepts either Kekulé drawing;
  the generator adds benzene derivatives (~a tenth of `--count`) — met (oracle 491 pass, 30
  of them benzenes).

## Decisions / deviations

- Traditional names: `tolueno` and `estireno` are listed under "Otras formas válidas"
  (P-22.1.3). `tolueno` is labelled as the name IUPAC 2013 keeps as preferred, even though
  `metilbenceno` is shown first. `cumeno` is not offered because 2013 no longer retains it
  (see the fixture justification).
- On wide mouse screens, the ring buttons are 1.75rem tall so the seventh button does not
  push Deshacer below the fold at 1280×720. Touch and phone layouts are unchanged.
- The "Nombra los sustituyentes" step no longer draws a locant the name leaves out (a lone
  substituent on a ring). This changes the metilciclohexano snapshots too.
- The worker used `git stash`/`pop` once to measure the toolbar; nothing was committed.

## Review

- Codex, ship-with-fixes — `docs/reviews/I-28.md`. Both should-fix findings are fixed with
  regressions:
  1. Benceno on an incompatible bond (isobutane C–C, a triple bond, a benzene single bond)
     silently made a ring that is not a benzene. It is now refused with "Aquí no cabe un
     benceno…" and no preview; unit tests cover the refused and accepted bonds.
  2. After Ordenar dibujo, `withOrderedMarks()` in `src/ui/results.js` drew all six ring
     locants because the benzene explanation has no numbering step. Locants now need an
     existing numbering step. An e2e test checks that an ordered toluene shows no ring numbers.
