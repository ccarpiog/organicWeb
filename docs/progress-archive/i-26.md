## Decisions

- Locant omission (design §1.1): unsubstituted ring with one multiple bond
  drops the locant (`ciclohexeno`, P-31.1.4.2.4); saturated ring with one
  substituent drops it (`metilciclohexano`, P-14.3.4.2(c)); everything else
  keeps every locant (`3-metilciclohex-1-eno`), the explanation noting some
  textbooks drop the 1.
- Benzene (6-ring, three alternating double bonds) stays refused with `CYCLE`,
  `ringReason: 'benzene'`, until I-28 (P-22.1.2 retains "benceno");
  cyclooctatetraene is named normally. `CYCLE_MESSAGES` removed.
- Side chains over 30 C → `TOO_BIG`.
