## Decisions and deviations (worker)

- `ciclohex-1-eno` → `otherName` («ciclohexeno»): the forward engine offers no located form of a
  bare ring's one double bond.
- The "filled" comparison (§15.5.3 step 6) also tries leaving the parent's prefix locants or its
  multiple-bond locants unwritten — otherwise `(buten-3-in-1-il)ciclopropano` could never match a
  monosubstituted ring, which omits its prefix locant.
- Names whose molecule has no structural key (three rings, two rings bonded directly:
  `ciclohexilciclohexano`) → `outOfScope` with the engine's "several rings" message, before any
  search (`canonicalKey()` would throw).
- Several structures, all refused (`diciclohexilpropano`) → `outOfScope` with no molecule, not
  `ambiguous`.
- Crowded prefixes: a ring branch after the first of two or more locant-less prefixes is enclosed
  (`metil(ciclohexil)metano`), as render.js `enclosedInName()` does.
- Closing-bond locants (`ciclopropa-1,1(3),2-trieno`) cannot be built from tiles yet (R-5a's `1(6)`
  control); the closed loop skips the one such name.
- `hexametilciclohexano` → `ambiguous` (18 structures) in ~20 ms; `dodecametilciclohexano` →
  `missingLocants` within budget.
- New Spanish drafts (pending teacher review): row «Anillos»; errors «Después de «ciclo» va una
  raíz…», «Un anillo tiene por lo menos 3 carbonos…», «…si es el anillo principal…», «El anillo de
  6 carbonos va del 1 al 6…», «El anillo de la rama va del…», «…el anillo de 3 carbonos solo tiene
  3 enlaces entre carbonos.»
