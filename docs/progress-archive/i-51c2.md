## What was built

- **Tile design (decision):** the acyl ending stands in the branch's free-valence place, after the branch's own ending, as the
  engine derives acyl prefixes from the acid's name (`-oico` → `-oil`, `-carboxílico` → `-carbonil`): `oil` after a chain's `ano` or
  last segment (`prop ano oil` → `propanoil`, `but 2 eno oil` → `(but-2-enoil)`, `START 2 met il prop ano oil END` →
  `(2-metilpropanoil)`), `carbonil` after a `ciclo` ring's ending, with the locant 1 where the engine writes it (`ciclo hex ano
  carbonil` → `(ciclohexanocarbonil)`, `ciclo hex 2 eno 1 carbonil` → `(ciclohex-2-eno-1-carbonil)`); in the tree the tile is the chain
  node's `fv`, so every rule that reads a branch reads an acyl branch too. `acetil` and `benzoil` are whole retained words, the engine's
  retained acyl prefixes; `benzoil`, like `fenil`, is the own chain of a compound branch with prefixes of its own (`START 4 met il
  benzoil END` → `(4-metilbenzoil)`, carbons 2…6). Rejected: whole-word tiles per acyl group (they would not compose with substituted
  or unsaturated chains, nor teach the acid-name derivation), and an `oil` that brings its own `an` (`prop oil`): the student writes
  the chain's ending as in the acid's name, and the app only elides its vowel.
- **Parser:** `oil` without the branch's ending, `oil` on a ring, `carbonil` on a chain, numbers before `oil`, two numbers before
  `carbonil`, an acyl ending alone, `benceno carbonil` / `benceno oil`, `oxi` after an acyl branch (an ester's prefix, R-6a) and an acyl
  group in an ether's class name are TILE_ORDER (LOCANT_COUNT for the two numbers), each with its hint; `prop ano oil` alone is
  ENDS_WITH_BRANCH.
- **Assembler (rule 8):** the ending before `oil` loses its vowel (`propan` + `oil`, `but-2-en` + `oil`, `buta-2,3-dien` + `oil`) and
  keeps it before `carbonil` (`ciclohexano` + `carbonil`, `ciclohex-2-eno-1-carbonil`); a chain's acyl prefix is enclosed with a
  multiple bond or a cited locant (or prefixes of its own, as any compound branch), a ring's always, `acetil` and `benzoil` never by
  themselves; the student's multiplier stays as chosen (`diacetil`, `di(prop-2-enoil)`, `bis(2-metilpropanoil)`); nesting as render.js
  (`[4-(propan-2-il)benzoil]`). An acyl word keeps its `il` before `oxi` (render.js's `(acetiloxi)`, `(formiloxi)`; out of place
  until R-6a).
- **Interpreter / builder / search:** SubstituentSpec `acyl`. A chain's acyl prefix is the chain bonded by its carbon 1 (implicit on
  the `oil` tile), its =O built on that carbon (two bonds, blamed on the `oil` tile, VALENCE note «…: el grupo C=O de «oil» va en ese
  carbono.»), counted in the search model; a ring's (`carbonil`, `benzoil`) is the ring with a new C=O carbon single-bonded to its
  free-valence carbon (one more carbon, two more heavy atoms), the occurrence's attachment; `acetil` a retained template. Two rings
  through the C=O carbon are built and the engine refuses them (`benzoilbenceno` → outOfScope, several rings); a benzene with a C=O on
  the ring and on a branch too (`4-acetilbenzaldehído`, `1,4-diacetilbenceno`) gets the engine's refusal.
- **Diagnosis:** acyl branches have the engine's acyl shapes for the prefix style; BRANCH_AT_END, LONGER_CHAIN, MORE_SUBSTITUENTS
  (now «sustituyentes» beside an acyl or `formil` prefix, whose C=O is an `oxo` of a chain through its carbon), CHAIN_LOCANTS,
  NUMBERING, ALPHABETICAL_ORDER, UNGROUPED, MULTIPLIER_KIND and NEEDLESS_LOCANTS (the locant 1 before `carbonil`) hold and were checked
  message by message.
- **Dialog:** the four tiles with tooltips, `oil` and `carbonil` in the prefix colour (`.nb-tile--acyl`).

## Decisions

- **`etanoil` and `metanoil` are read** (IUPAC writes them; the engine writes `acetil` and `formil`): `2-etanoilheptanal` →
  otherName «2-acetilheptanal» (null diagnosis), `2-metanoilheptanal` → otherName «2-pentilpropanodial» (the –CHO joins the chain).
- **A free-valence locant other than 1 before `carbonil` is read** as `il`'s is on a ring (`(ciclohexano-2-carbonil)` → otherName,
  null); `oil` takes none (TILE_ORDER), its C=O always the branch's carbon 1.
- **An acyl prefix on a hydrocarbon ring, `benceno` or a template is read** and judged: the molecule is the ketone the engine names
  from the chain (`acetilbenceno` → «1-feniletan-1-ona», with its «acetofenona»; `butanoilciclohexano` → «1-ciclohexilbutan-1-ona»).
- **Fixed during the sweep:** NEEDLESS_LOCANTS on `(ciclohexano-1-carbonil)` quoted «(ciclohexcarbonil)» (shortForm() dropped the `ano`
  as for `(propan-1-il)`); now «(ciclohexanocarbonil)», and never on a ring with a multiple bond or prefixes of its own, where the
  engine writes the 1.
- **Deliberate, tested nulls** (diagnose.js «Aldehydes and ketones», for I-51c3): an acyl prefix in words the engine does not write
  (`etanoil` for `acetil`; `(1-oxoetil)`, `(1-oxopropil)` for the acyl word; its own chain or numbering otherwise —
  `(3-metilpropanoil)`, `(2-etilpropanoil)`, `(6-metilbenzoil)`, `(6-metilciclohexano-1-carbonil)`, `(ciclohexano-2-carbonil)` —; the
  locant 1 before `carbonil` left out, `(ciclohex-2-enocarbonil)`); an acyl prefix where the engine's parent is the ketone's chain
  (`acetilbenceno`, `butanoilciclohexano`, `4-acetiltolueno`) or moved to an end carbon whose C=O the chain then takes
  (`1-acetilhexanal` → «octano-2,3-diona»; 15 of the fixture sweep's nulls, 340 of the generated one); and, as in I-51c1, a ring
  numbered otherwise at N0 (`5-acetilciclohexan-2-ona`) or the –CHO of a two-carbon chain put on the ketone's carbon
  (`1-(…)-2-oxoetanal`). The I-51c1 nulls stand.
