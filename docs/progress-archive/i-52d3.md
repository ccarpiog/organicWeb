## What was built

- **Tiles (tile order, after `bencilamina`):** `formamida` (metanamida), `acetamida` (etanamida), `ftalamida`
  (benceno-1,2-dicarboxamida). Ids `template-formamide`, `template-acetamide`, `template-phthalamide`; texts from the lexicon
  (TRADITIONAL_NAMES, BENZENE_DIGROUP_NAMES).
- **Model:** each is a template whose `suffix` places the –CONH₂ groups, as `acético` places its –COOH: SUFFIX_GROUPS `amide` on the
  chain templates (the amide's carbon the chain's carbon 1, its =O and its nitrogen on it), `carboxamide` on `ftalamida` (two new
  carbons outside the ring at 1 and 2). interpret.js gives a placed amide suffix `amideNitrogen`, so `nitrogenSlots()` gives the
  prefixes of a one-amide word their `N` slots exactly as on an `amida` suffix (no new code path); build.js `attachSuffix()` puts the
  =O and the nitrogen on each group's carbon, the first one's nitrogen taking the `N` groups; `polyamideRefusal()` refuses an `N`
  beside `ftalamida`'s two groups; search.js counts the nitrogen's bonds at index N_VALUE.
- **What the engine admits (`templateAdmits()`):** `formamida` and `acetamida` with any prefixes all written `N` (`nitrogenPrefixes`),
  `acetamida` with one phenyl (bare or substituted) instead, never both (principal.js: `onNitrogen`, `phenylOnly`), `ftalamida` with any
  ring prefixes (`substitutable`).
- **Parser:** TEMPLATE_LOCANTS and POSITIONAL as for the other templates, «grupo amida» / «grupos amida» (PLACED_GROUP_WORDS);
  `checkNitrogen()` keeps `N` to an amide template's name-level prefixes; an amide template in a compound branch or an O-bound side is
  TILE_ORDER with a hint saying how the engine writes an amide there (`amino` + `oxo` on a carbon of the branch, or `carbamoil`).
- **Diagnosis:** SUBSTITUTED_TEMPLATE (chain wording) for prefixes the engine never writes, its message saying what the word admits;
  the ring and branch rules on the prefixes it admits; the shared rules on the groups on the nitrogen; amideChecks() (first) gives the
  templates' own mistakes their «Amides» null categories.
- **Ayuda:** the intro sentence ends «…, y de sus nitrilos y aminas, y de sus amidas.»; a new «Amidas» paragraph (`amida`,
  `carboxamida`, `benzamida`, `N`, `amino` + `oxo` and `carbamoil`, acylamino, the retained names and what each admits).

## Decisions

- **Only the names the engine writes:** `formamida`, `acetamida` (with groups on the N; or a phenyl, bare or substituted, on carbon 2),
  `ftalamida` (with ring prefixes). aromatic.js gives the amide entry of `retainedDigroupIds()` the 1,2 position only (no
  `isoftalamida` / `tereftalamida`), and principal.js has no retained ethanediamide (no `oxamida`; etanodiamida has no traditional
  alternative, tested). `benzamida` was already a tile («Anillos», R-6d1).
- **No `fenilacetamida` tile.** Unlike `fenilacetonitrilo` / `fenilacético`, the engine's word is `2-fenilacetamida` with its locant
  (lexicon.es.js: the N could carry the phenyl too). The student builds `2`, `fenil`, `acetamida`, which assemble to the engine's text and
  read as its alternative; `fenilacetamida` without the locant is `ambiguous` (2-fenil or N-fenil, both engine names) — the reason the
  engine keeps the locant. A `2-fenilacetamida` tile would carry a locant inside a tile, which no tile does.
- **`nitrogenPrefixes` as a TEMPLATES flag**, beside `arylPrefix` and `substitutable`: the engine admits any groups on the N of
  `formamida` / `acetamida` (N-alkyl, N-aryl, N-substituted groups, `N-(cianometil)…`) and nothing on their carbons except the phenyl.
- **SUBSTITUTED_TEMPLATE says what an amide word admits.** The shared chain message «…no admite sustituyentes…» would be false for
  `acetamida` (`N-metilacetamida` is the engine's), so the code keeps its rule and step and, for a `nitrogenPrefixes` word, says «solo
  admite grupos en el nitrógeno, con «N» (N-metilacetamida), o solo un fenil en el carbono 2 (2-fenilacetamida): con otros grupos…»
  (`formamida` without the phenyl clause). The other families' chain message is unchanged (their `arylPrefix` imprecision is
  pre-existing).
- **Verdicts follow the engine:** every retained form is an `alternative`; the systematic names stay `correct` and announce it.
- **No class word, no O-bound side:** the amide templates are the nitrile templates' twins; `ácido acetamida`, `acetamida de metilo` and
  a suffix after the word get the existing ACID_WORD / ESTER_SUFFIX / TILE_ORDER messages. An `N` alone before the word
  (`N-acetamida`) is TEMPLATE_LOCANTS, as before `anilina` and `benzamida`. No new error code.
- **Sweeps apart (I-52b2 / I-52c3 precedent):** the earlier amide files keep their names and exact counts (`isR6d1AmideName()` /
  `isR6d2AmideName()` exclude the retained names); the template names are swept in the new file with mutations that fit them.
- **Chemistry:** nothing from memory; every name, label, admitted form and refusal is the forward engine's.
