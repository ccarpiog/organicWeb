# I-40a — Rings as substituents of a chain carrying the principal group (split from I-40)

- Risk: high. Worker: opus. I-40 was split into I-40a (this phase), I-40b (`-carboxílico`,
  `-carbaldehído`, `-carbonitrilo`, `-carboxamida`, ring acyl prefixes) and I-40c (ring esters);
  design §13.4 records the three rows.

## Acceptance

- The side-chain refusals `sideChainAlcohol`, `sideChainCarbonyl` and `sideChainAmine` are lifted
  (`oxygenPlacementError()` keeps `ringAldehyde`; `aminePlacementError()` and the three messages are
  gone) — met.
- Examples named: `2-ciclohexiletan-1-ol`, `1-feniletan-1-ol`, `fenilmetanol` (with `alcohol
  bencílico`), `1-fenilpropan-1-ona`, `1-ciclohexiletan-1-ona`, `1-feniletan-1-ona` (with
  `acetofenona`), `fenilmetanamina` (with `bencilamina`), `2-feniletan-1-amina` — met.
- Ring prefixes: `ciclopropil`…`ciclotriacontil`, `fenil`, substituted (`(2-metilciclohexil)`,
  `(4-hidroxiciclohexil)`), unsaturated (`(ciclohex-2-en-1-il)`), doubly bonded (`ciclohexiliden`),
  on an ether O (`fenoxi`, `(ciclohexiloxi)`) and on an amino N (`(ciclohexilamino)`) — met.
- Explanation step «Anillo o cadena», snapshots, fixtures (34 rows), unit tests
  (`tests/unit/ring-substituents.test.js`), e2e (`tests/e2e/ring-substituents.spec.js`), oracle
  generator `generateRingSubstituents()` — met. Ordenar dibujo draws the ring prefix as a polygon.

## Decisions (IUPAC 2013; from memory where marked)

- Parent choice: P-44.1.1 (most principal groups) before P-44.1.2.2 (ring senior on a tie).
  `parent.js ringOrChain()` counts suffix groups on ring atoms against the best chain of the skeleton
  without the ring atoms; an amine N bonded to a ring carbon counts for the ring, so every I-36 ring
  amine (`N-metilbencenamina`) is unchanged and a ring is never an `N-` prefix of a principal amine
  chain. Trace step `RINGCHAIN` first (chain parent, or ring on a tie with a group off the ring).
- Ring carbons are never chain carbons (parent or branch); ring prefix numbered from the attachment
  atom by the ring cascade.
- `fenoxi` retained contracted; cycloalkoxy never contracted (`ciclopropiloxi`).
- `(fenilamino)`, not `anilino` (IUPAC 2013 may prefer `anilino`; from memory, not offered — like
  `acetamido` in I-39d).
- A ring prefix cited after another locant-less prefix is enclosed (`cloro(fenil)metanol`); cited
  first it is not (`fenil(metoxi)metanol`) — app convention.
- `acetofenona`, `alcohol bencílico`, `bencilamina` as "acepta" traditional names for the bare
  molecules (status from memory).

## Refusals

- New engine refusals: `symmetricRing` (identical principal branches on a ring: multiplicative
  `ciclohexano-1,4-diildimetanol`, from memory) and `ringAcyl` (acyl carbon on the ring:
  `ciclohexanocarbonil`, `benzoil`, for I-40b).
- Unchanged: `ringAcid`, `ringAldehyde`, `ringNitrile`, `ringAmide` (I-40b), `ringEster` (I-40c).

## Open points

- English oracle names keep the Spanish citation order (`1-phenyl-N-methylmethanamine`); OPSIN checks
  structure only.
- Explanation option order among tied chains follows atom ids (pre-existing pattern).
