## Decisions

- `ácido prop-2-enoico` keeps its locant (with a suffix the locant is written, as `prop-2-enal`).
- An aldehyde end next to an acid principal is `oxo-` (`ácido 3-oxopropanoico`, IUPAC 2013).
- `ácido fórmico`, `ácido acético`, `ácido oxálico` offered under "Otras formas válidas",
  labelled like `tolueno` (retained/preferred in IUPAC 2013, from memory); no other
  traditional names.
- "ácido" is its own name part, highlighted with the whole group.
- `carboxy-` branches cannot pass validation (two COOH are always chain ends);
  `carboxySubstituent` is a tested-indirectly safety net.
- The acid oracle generator keeps only molecules named in all three prefix styles (the
  'substituted' style may need a refused `formil` branch).
- Older tests that used acetic acid as a refused example now use an ester or acid + amine.
