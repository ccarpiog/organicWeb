## Decisions

- Final `o` of the parent elided before `-ol`, kept before a consonant multiplier
  (`propan-2-ol`, `etano-1,2-diol`); the suffix multiplier drops its `a` before `ol`
  (`tetrol`, `pentol`; P-16.7.1(c)); prefixes unaffected (`tetrametil`).
- Locants from memory: `2-cloroetan-1-ol` (locants cited once there are two substituents;
  ethanol-style omission only with one). Enols (`etenol`) and gem-diols
  (`propano-2,2-diol`) named systematically. No traditional names (the design asks none).
- A ring with an OH on a side chain (`OCC1=CC=CC=C1`) is refused with its own message
  until I-40 (needs `ciclohexil`/`fenil` prefixes there).
- Ring closing bond (atoms n–1): ranks as locant n when comparing numberings (keeps
  `ciclonona-1,2-dieno`, `ciclohex-2-eno-1,2-diol`), and is rendered with the compound
  locant `1(n)` when the OH locants force it (`ciclohex-1(6)-eno-1,2,4-triol`); OPSIN parses it.
- "Show OH": O atoms were already labelled OH; explanations now highlight each OH and its carbon.
- New explain steps "Reconoce el grupo funcional" and "Busca la cadena principal";
  Ayuda and refusal text mention alcohols.
