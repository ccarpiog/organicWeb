# I-32 — v2.12 Aldehydes and ketones

- Spec: design §13.4 row I-32, §13.6; `autoclaude/processed/215-rings-functional-groups-confirmed.md` §3 item 12.
- Risk class: high. Worker: opus (one general-purpose worker); review fix applied by the orchestrator.

## Acceptance

- Met. `metanal`, `etanal`, `propanona` (alternatives `propan-2-ona`, `acetona`), `butan-2-ona`,
  `butanodial`, `pentano-2,4-diona`, `4-oxopentanal`, `3-hidroxibutanal`,
  `4-hidroxibutan-2-ona`, `pent-3-en-2-ona`, `2-cloroetanal`, `2,2-dimetilbutanal`,
  `ciclohexanona` checked by hand from SMILES; CHO on a ring and acids stay `HETEROATOM`.
- Out-of-scope carbonyl patterns are refused with a reason (`ringAldehyde`,
  `sideChainCarbonyl`, `manyAldehydes`, `acylSubstituent`), never misnamed.

## Decisions

- New `src/naming/principal.js` (oxygen kind, principal kind, traditional ids). Principal
  group decides chain and numbering before length (I-31's P0/N0 generalised to C=O).
  C=O is never a chain unsaturation.
- Cycloalkanones brought forward from I-40 (the I-31 ring machinery handles them); any
  aldehyde on a ring molecule (carbaldehído) and a ketone on a ring's side chain are
  refused until I-40.
- Branch carbonyls: `oxo-` inside a branch (`4-(2-oxopropil)heptano-2,6-diona`); `formil-`
  never needed, not implemented. Acyl-type branches (C=O carbon bonded directly to the
  parent, e.g. `3-acetilpentano-2,4-diona`) and more than two CHO are refused.
- Traditional names: `acetona`, plus `formaldehído` / `acetaldehído` for bare metanal /
  etanal only (IUPAC 2013 retains them; usual school names).
- `propanona` without locant per the design; the 2013 PIN `propan-2-ona` is offered as an
  alternative (style `locants`). `2-cloroetanal` keeps its locant (follows I-31's
  `2-cloroetan-1-ol`). IUPAC rule numbers cited from memory.
- One group snapshot (`OCCC(=O)CC=O`, now nameable) replaced by an amine variant.
