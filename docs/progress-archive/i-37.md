## Decisions

- Diamides (`butanodiamida`, `etanodiamida`) are named like diacids; a diamide with any group on an
  N is refused (`substitutedPolyamide`, N¹/N⁴ locants), mirroring I-36.
- Two amides whose carbons lie on different carbon pieces (joined through an N or O) are refused as
  `amidePrefix` (one would be `acilamino-`), even where IUPAC 2013 would use a multiplicative name
  (`2,2′-azanodiildiacetamida`); a third amide is `manyAmides`.
- Any amide with a ring is refused (`ringAmide`), including `N-fenil…` (not trivially supported:
  the ring would be a group on the N, I-40). Lactams stay `RING_SYSTEM`.
- An N bonded to two C=O carbons is an imide with its own message (`imide`), not an N-acyl amide.
  Ureas, carbamates, hydrazides, N-halo and N–O amides keep the generic HETEROATOM message.
- Groups on the N follow I-36: prefixes with locant `N`, never counted for locant omission; when an
  `N` is written, carbon locants are too (`2-cloro-N-metiletanamida`).
- One-carbon parents (`metanamida`, `N,N-dimetilmetanamida`) get no "Busca la cadena principal"
  step, like `metanol`; the group step says the amide carbon is the only chain carbon.

## Decisions (from memory, not checked online)

- IUPAC 2013 retains formamide and acetamide as preferred names (P-66.1.1.1.1) and allows
  substitution on the N (`N,N-dimetilformamida`, `N-metilacetamida`) but not on the acyl part;
  offered under "Otras formas válidas" only (v2 rule "systematic name first"). No other retained
  amide names (propionamida, benzamida…).
- Seniority amide below ester, above nitrile / aldehyde (P-41).
