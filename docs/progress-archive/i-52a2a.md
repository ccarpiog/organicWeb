## What was built

- **Tiles (tile order, after `tereftálico`):** `formiato` (a metanoato), `acetato` (an etanoato), `fenilacetato` (a
  2-feniletanoato). Ids `template-formate`, `template-acetate`, `template-phenylacetate` (the TEMPLATES key is the lexicon's
  TRADITIONAL_NAMES id); texts from the lexicon (`TRADITIONAL_NAMES.formate` …).
- **Model:** each is a chain template whose `suffix` places the ester's –COO– at carbon 1 (SUFFIX_GROUPS `ester`, `bridge`):
  interpret.js reads it as `acético` with kind 'ester' and `bridge`, build.js puts the =O and the bridge oxygen on carbon 1 (the
  chain's own, «…en el acetato, ese carbono ya lleva un grupo COO»), and the O-bound side after it is read, built and bonded to
  the bridge oxygen exactly as after `oato` (I-52a1). `fenilacetato`'s phenyl is its template group at 2; `acetato` admits one
  substituted phenyl on carbon 2 (`arylPrefix`, the engine's 'arylacetate', the same word).
- **Parser:** `esterSuffixOf()` gives an ester template one –COO– (so one O-bound group; ESTER_GROUPS otherwise, with the
  template's own example); TEMPLATE_LOCANTS and POSITIONAL as for the other chain templates, «grupo COO»; ACID_WORD for `ácido`
  before an ester word, and an ESTER_SUFFIX variant for an O-bound side after `ácido fórmico` / `acético` / `fenilacético` that
  names the ester's word; the TILE_ORDER hint of an ester word in a branch or in the O-bound side.
- **Tokenizer:** class TPLEST where TPL stands (at the start, after a complete prefix or locants), followed by exactly ` de `;
  `fenilacetato` is read as its one tile (the longer surface form).
- **Diagnosis:** nothing new in the rules: SUBSTITUTED_TEMPLATE (chain wording, the template quoted as on its tile) for prefixes
  the engine never writes; BRANCH_RING_NUMBERING on `acetato`'s substituted phenyl; traditionalLocants()' NEEDLESS_LOCANTS for
  `2-fenilacetato de metilo`. `esterSideDiffers()` compares the O-bound side of a retained ester word with the engine's
  traditional name when it gives one (the engine writes it in the default style only), so `acetato de propan-2-ilo` is an
  `oBound` null like `acetato de propan-1-ilo`.
- **Ayuda:** the intro sentence ends «…, y de sus ácidos carboxílicos y ésteres.»; a new «Ésteres» paragraph (`oato`,
  `carboxilato`, `benzoato`, `ilo`, the automatic «de» and «y», `di` on the O-bound side, the retained names and which takes
  prefixes).

## Decisions

- **Verdicts follow the engine:** every retained form is an `alternative` (the engine lists it last, never first); the systematic
  names stay `correct` and announce it («¡Correcto! También se llama «acetato de etilo» (…)»).
- **The retained word comes with the engine's default O-bound style only.** The engine writes `acetato de isopropilo`, never
  `acetato de propan-2-ilo` / `acetato de 1-metiletilo` (its pin and substituted alternatives are systematic: `etanoato de
  propan-2-ilo`). So the exact comparison makes `acetato de propan-2-ilo` an `otherName`; rather than leave it a stray null,
  `esterSideDiffers()` now compares a retained ester word's O-bound side with the engine's traditional name, which makes it a
  deliberate «Esters» `oBound` null for I-52a3 (the main mistake is the mix). Without a traditional name (the student's prefixes
  on the word) it compares with every style as before, so `2-cloroacetato de propan-2-ilo` still gets SUBSTITUTED_TEMPLATE.
- **`fenilacetato` is its own tile**, following `fenilacético` / `fenilacetaldehído`; `fenil` + `acetato` writes the same text and
  reads as the same alternative.
- **Pairing messages:** ACID_WORD and ESTER_SUFFIX are reused with new message variants (no new code): `ácido acetato` says the
  ester's acid part is written without `ácido`; `ácido acético de metilo` says the acid's ester word (`acetato`), from the new
  `ACID_ESTER_TEMPLATES` map; `ácido oxálico de dimetilo` (no retained ester word: the engine writes `etanodioato de dimetilo`)
  keeps the general ESTER_SUFFIX message.
- **`esterSuffixOf()` sets `template` only for an ester template**, so the results of `oato`, `carboxilato` and `benzoato` keep
  their shape (their deepEqual tests stand).
- **Sweeps split, not merged (as I-51d2):** the R-6a1 sweeps keep their names and exact counts; the retained names are swept apart
  with mutations that fit them (a methyl on the template's free carbon, `fenilacetato` split as `2 fenil acetato`, the aryl
  phenyl reflected, the O-bound side in the engine's other styles, an O-bound phenyl reflected).
- **Chemistry:** nothing from memory; every name, label and admitted form is the forward engine's.

## Deliberate nulls («Esters», for I-52a3)

- `carbon`: a retained word whose –COO– carbon the engine puts outside a ring — `fenilformiato de metilo` → `benzoato de
  metilo`, `(4-clorofenil)formiato de metilo` → `4-clorobenzoato de metilo` (esterChecks()).
- `oBound`: an O-bound side the engine does not write beside the retained word — another style (`acetato de propan-2-ilo`,
  `acetato de 1-metiletilo`, `formiato de propan-2-ilo`), or a group the engine never writes, as after `oato` (`acetato de
  propan-1-ilo`, `acetato de 1,2-dimetilpropilo`, `acetato de 6-metilfenilo`) (esterSideDiffers()).
- **Counts:** NULLS test 8 cases (2 `carbon`, 6 `oBound`); fixture template sweep 9 `oBound` of 51 `otherName`; generated template
  sweep 54 `oBound` of 253 (10 in another style, 44 a phenyl reflected). The R-6a1 counts are unchanged: fixture 94 of 155 (90
  `numbering`, 4 `oBound`), generated 407 of 1040 (384 `numbering`, 23 `oBound`), carbonyl fixture 8 (`seniority`).

## Deviations / open risks

- **Open question (not an «Esters» null, pre-existing pattern):** an aryl prefix in another style than the engine's default beside
  a retained word (`2-[4-(propan-2-il)fenil]acetato de metilo`) is `otherName` with a null diagnosis, as on the retained aldehyde
  and acid since R-5c3a / R-5d2 (`2-[4-(propan-2-il)fenil]acetaldehído`, `ácido 2-[4-(propan-2-il)fenil]acético`,
  `4-(propan-2-il)ftalaldehído`): the engine writes its retained names in the default prefix style only, and no rule speaks of a
  retained word's prefix style. Tested as it is; not in any sweep (no mutation changes the acid part's style).
- `cloroformiato de metilo` builds ClC(=O)OCH₃, which the engine refuses (a carbonic acid's ester, HETEROATOM): `outOfScope` with
  the engine's message, as the brief allows.
- No forward-engine bug found; nothing in `src/naming/` or `src/explain/` changed.
- design.md §15.8's Status column is not updated (the user's uncommitted file).
