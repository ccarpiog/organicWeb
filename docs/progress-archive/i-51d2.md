## What was built

- **Tiles (tile order, after `tereftalaldehído`):** `fórmico` (ácido metanoico), `acético` (ácido etanoico), `oxálico`
  (ácido etanodioico), `fenilacético` (ácido 2-feniletanoico), `ftálico`, `isoftálico`, `tereftálico` (ácido
  benceno-1,2/1,3/1,4-dicarboxílico). Ids `template-formicAcid` … `template-terephthalicAcid` (the TEMPLATES key is the
  lexicon id: TRADITIONAL_NAMES for the chain ones, BENZENE_DIGROUP_NAMES for the ftálicos); texts from the lexicon without
  the class word, which is its own tile (the I-51d1 `suffixClass` slot), so prefixes go between: `ácido 4-metilftálico`.
- **Model:** each is a carbonyl template whose `suffix` places the –COOH groups: SUFFIX_GROUPS `acid` on the chain templates
  (`fórmico` 1 carbon, `acético`, `oxálico`, `fenilacético` 2; the –COOH carbon at 1, `oxálico`'s at 1 and 2), `carboxylic`
  on the ftálicos (outside the ring, at 1 and 2 / 3 / 4). interpret.js, build.js (the =O and the OH of each –COOH), search.js
  and the verdict's `suffixAtoms` treat them as the engine's principal group with no new code path.
- **Parser:** ACID_WORD both ways through `acidSuffixTile()` (an acid template is an acid suffix); TEMPLATE_LOCANTS and
  POSITIONAL as for the carbonyl templates, with «grupo COOH» / «grupos COOH» and the template quoted with `ácido`; the
  compound-branch hint says the –COOH is `carboxi` there.
- **Messages quote the name as written:** `parentWordName()` gives «ácido acético», used by the TemplateSpec word, the placed
  suffix's word and ownGroups(), so LOCANT_RANGE, VALENCE, TEMPLATE_LOCANTS, POSITIONAL and SUBSTITUTED_TEMPLATE say «ácido
  acético» («La cadena del ácido acético tiene 2 carbonos…»).
- **Diagnosis:** nothing new: SUBSTITUTED_TEMPLATE (chain wording) for prefixes the engine never writes; on the ftálicos and
  a phenyl on `acético` (templateAdmits()) the other rules (RING_NUMBERING, BRANCH_RING_NUMBERING); traditionalLocants()'s
  NEEDLESS_LOCANTS for `ácido 2-fenilacético`; acidChecks() first, so a template whose –COOH groups are not the engine's
  stays an acid null.
- **Ayuda:** the intro sentence ends «…, y también de sus éteres, aldehídos y cetonas, y de sus ácidos carboxílicos.»; a new
  «Ácidos» paragraph (`ácido` first, `oico`, `carboxílico`, `ácido benzoico`, `carboxi`, the retained names and which take
  prefixes).

## Decisions

- **`fenilacético` is its own tile**, following `fenilacetaldehído` (the engine's one word); `fenil` + `acético` writes the
  same text and reads as the same alternative; the tokenizer returns the single tile (longest surface form first).
- **Admitted prefixes from the code, not memory:** the engine writes `fórmico`, `oxálico` and `fenilacético` bare only, the
  ftálicos with any prefixes (aromatic.js `substitutable`) and `acético` with one substituted phenyl on carbon 2
  (`arylaceticAcid`), so `templateAdmits()` needed only the flags (`substitutable`, `arylPrefix`).
- **ACID_WORD reused** (no new code): the missing-`ácido` message now gives the student's own word as its example for a
  template (`como en ácido acético`), and `ácido` before a template that is no acid's gets a variant naming the retained acid
  words; the generic messages for `oico`, `carboxílico`, `benzoico` are unchanged.
- **`isCarbonylTemplate()` excludes the acid templates** (new `isAcidTemplate()`), so the carbonyl branch hint never speaks of
  `oxo` / `formil` for an acid.
- **Sweeps split, not merged:** the R-5d1 names are swept with their exact earlier counts (the generated sweep's total and
  nulls now asserted exactly: 911, `numbering` 380) and the template names apart, so a regression of the earlier names cannot
  hide behind new mutations. `mutationsOf()` now numbers a chain template on its own chain (`reversed`, n = its carbons)
  instead of as a ring of six; no R-5d1 name has a template parent, so their mutations are unchanged.
- **Chemistry:** nothing from memory; every name, label and admitted form is the forward engine's.

## Deviations / open risks

- None of the retained names is the engine's first name, so all are `alternative` (as the brief allows); the systematic names
  are `correct` and announce them («¡Correcto! También se llama «ácido acético» (…)»).
- A prefix on `oxálico` never builds (both carbons are full): with a locant VALENCE («…en el ácido oxálico, ese carbono ya
  lleva un grupo COOH»), without one `invalid` «No caben…» from the search — not `otherName`.
- `ácido 2,2-difenilacético` is `outOfScope` (two rings), as `2,2-difenilacetaldehído` would be.
- A template inside a compound branch (`ácido (acético)metano…`) gets two TILE_ORDER errors, the template's and its END's (a
  branch without its own chain), as the carbonyl templates do.
- design.md §15.8's Status column is not updated (the user's uncommitted file).
