## What was built

- **Tiles (tile order, after `fenilacetato`):** `acetonitrilo` (etanonitrilo), `fenilacetonitrilo` (2-feniletanonitrilo),
  `ftalonitrilo` (benceno-1,2-dicarbonitrilo). Ids `template-acetonitrile`, `template-phenylacetonitrile`, `template-phthalonitrile`
  (the TEMPLATES key is the lexicon id: TRADITIONAL_NAMES for the chain ones, BENZENE_DIGROUP_NAMES for `ftalonitrilo`); texts
  from the lexicon.
- **Model:** each is a template whose `suffix` places the –C≡N groups, as `acético` places its –COOH: SUFFIX_GROUPS `nitrile` on
  the chain templates (the –C≡N's carbon the chain's carbon 1, its nitrogen triple-bonded to it), `carbonitrile` on
  `ftalonitrilo` (two new carbons outside the ring at 1 and 2). interpret.js, build.js (attachSuffix() by element and order,
  R-6b1), search.js and the verdict's `suffixAtoms` treat them as the engine's principal group with no new code path.
  `fenilacetonitrilo`'s phenyl is its template group at 2; `acetonitrilo` admits one substituted phenyl on carbon 2 (`arylPrefix`,
  the engine's 'arylacetonitrile', the same word); `ftalonitrilo` admits any prefixes (`substitutable`).
- **Parser:** TEMPLATE_LOCANTS and POSITIONAL as for the other templates, «grupo C≡N» / «grupos C≡N» (PLACED_GROUP_WORDS, the
  entry I-52b1 left for this phase); the TILE_ORDER hint of a nitrile template in a compound branch or an O-bound side says the
  –C≡N is `ciano` there; `ácido` before the word and an O-bound side after it get the existing ACID_WORD / ESTER_SUFFIX messages.
- **Builder:** LOCANT_RANGE on `fenilacetonitrilo` adds that its ring is not numbered and gives `2-(4-metilfenil)etanonitrilo`,
  which the app reads (RING_TEMPLATE_EXAMPLES); VALENCE says «…en el acetonitrilo, ese carbono ya lleva un grupo C≡N».
- **Diagnosis:** nothing new in the rules (header «Nitriles» documents the templates): SUBSTITUTED_TEMPLATE (chain wording) for
  prefixes the engine never writes, the ring and branch rules on the prefixes it admits, traditionalLocants() for
  `2-fenilacetonitrilo`; nitrileChecks() first, so a template whose –C≡N groups are not the engine's is a «Nitriles» null.
- **Ayuda:** the intro sentence ends «…, y de sus ácidos carboxílicos y ésteres, y de sus nitrilos.»; a new «Nitrilos» paragraph
  (`nitrilo`, `carbonitrilo`, `benzonitrilo`, `ciano`, the retained names and which take prefixes).

## Decisions

- **Only the names the engine writes:** `acetonitrilo` (also `2-(aryl)acetonitrilo`), `fenilacetonitrilo`, `ftalonitrilo` (also
  substituted). aromatic.js gives the nitrile entry of `retainedDigroupIds()` the 1,2 position only, so no `isoftalonitrilo` or
  `tereftalonitrilo` tile (`isoftalonitrilo` and `tereftalonitrilo` are asserted untokenisable; the engine's names of the 1,3 and
  1,4 dinitriles have no traditional alternative, tested).
- **Verdicts follow the engine:** every retained form is an `alternative` (the engine lists it under «Otras formas válidas», never
  first); the systematic names stay `correct` and announce it («¡Correcto! También se llama «acetonitrilo» (…)»).
- **`fenilacetonitrilo` is its own tile**, following `fenilacético` / `fenilacetato` / `fenilacetaldehído`; `fenil` +
  `acetonitrilo` writes the same text and reads as the same alternative; the tokenizer returns the single tile.
- **No class word, no O-bound side:** the nitrile templates are the carbonyl templates' twins (plain TPL in the tokenizer, no
  ACID_WORD / ESTER pairing of their own); the malformed pairings get the existing generic messages (`ácido acetonitrilo`: the
  ACID_WORD message listing the retained acid words; `acetonitrilo de metilo`: the general ESTER_SUFFIX; a suffix after the word:
  the general TILE_ORDER after a parent), exactly as `acetaldehído al`, `ácido acético oico`, `benzonitrilo nitrilo` do. No new
  error code.
- **New `otherName` cases — which diagnosis and why:** a prefix the engine never writes gets SUBSTITUTED_TEMPLATE (the twins' rule;
  nitrileChecks() returns null there, the student's –C≡N groups being the engine's). A template whose –C≡N groups are not the
  engine's is a deliberate «Nitriles» null pinned for I-52b3, because nitrileChecks() runs first (R-6b1 decision) and every
  existing message would speak of the wrong group: another –C≡N written `ciano` beside the word (`count`: `2-cianoacetonitrilo` →
  propanodinitrilo, `4-cianoftalonitrilo` → benceno-1,2,4-tricarbonitrilo, `2-ciano` on `fenilacetonitrilo` →
  2-fenilpropanodinitrilo) and the word beside a senior group (`seniority`: `2-carboxiacetonitrilo` → ácido 2-cianoetanoico,
  `4-carboxiftalonitrilo` → ácido 3,4-dicianobenzoico) — the acids' twins of I-51d2, whose `ácido 2-carboxiacético` stayed an
  «Acids» null until I-51d3. No retained nitrile word can put the –C≡N's carbon on the other side of a parent (the engine has no
  `formonitrilo`), so no `carbon` case comes from them.
- **Sweeps split, not merged (I-51d2 / I-52a2a precedent):** reverse-nitriles.test.js sweeps the R-6b1 names with their exact
  earlier counts and the template names are swept apart with mutations that fit them (`substituted`: a methyl on the chain
  template's carbon 2; `phenylLocant`: `2 fenil acetonitrilo`; `arylReflected`: the aryl phenyl v → 8 − v; `ringReflected`:
  `ftalonitrilo`'s prefixes numbered from the other –C≡N, v → 9 − v; `cyano`: a `ciano` on a free carbon, the `count` null).
- **Chemistry:** nothing from memory; every name, label and admitted form is the forward engine's.
