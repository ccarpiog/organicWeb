## What was built

- **The ester checks** (`esterChecks()`, first in `firstRule()` beside acidChecks(), when the engine's principal group is an
  ester's; each rule 1, step `group`, «Reconoce el grupo funcional», which says the three atoms of a –COO– are one group, how it is
  written and, beside other oxygen groups, the seniority ácido > éster > aldehído > cetona > alcohol):
  - **ESTER_GROUP** (new, ACID_GROUP's twin) — an engine suffix –COO– whose carbon no ester suffix group and no ester prefix of the
    student's has (`studentEsterCarbons()`, the O-bound groups' branches too): its C=O and its bridge oxygen written apart
    (`1-metoxipropanal`, `1-metoxi-1-oxopropano`, `3-metoxi-3-oxopropan-1-ol`, `4-metoxi-4-oxobutanoato de metilo`,
    `[(metoxi)oxometil]benceno`, `(2-metoxi-2-oxoetil)ciclohexano`). Focus: the carbon, both oxygens and their bonds; tiles: the
    student's prefix holding one of the oxygens, else the suffix.
  - **CARBONYL_SENIORITY** (extended, `group` 'ester') — a junior suffix beside the –COO–; a junior group whose carbons are an
    O-bound group's goes «en el nombre del grupo unido al otro oxígeno» (`juniorPlacement()`). Beside an acid, a student's ester
    suffix is the junior `ester`: its C=O carbon in the engine's chain gives «con dos prefijos con el mismo número: «oxo» … y uno
    acabado en «-oxi» …», else the engine's ester prefix that holds it (`acetiloxi`).
  - **KETONE_NOT_ALDEHYDE** (reused) beside the –COO– (`3-(acetiloxi)-1-metilpropanal`).
  - **ESTER_CARBON** (new, ACID_CARBON's twin through `groupCarbon()`).
  - **CARBONYL_PREFIX** (extended, `esterPrefix()`) — a parent-level alkoxycarbonyl prefix (its carbon joins a chain) or an
    acyloxy prefix at any depth (the student's chain or ring goes into the O-bound group: «Tu cadena va en el grupo unido al otro
    oxígeno, después de «de».») holding an engine suffix –COO–, while the student's ester suffix cites fewer.
- **The rules with the –COO–'s words** (`group` 'ester' through CARBONYL_GROUPS): CARBONYL_PARENT (ring or chain), MORE_CARBONYL_GROUPS
  (P0), ETHER_SIDE told from either (`[2-(metoxicarbonil)etoxi]metano`), NUMBERING / RING_NUMBERING / CHAIN_LOCANTS at N0,
  NEEDLESS_LOCANTS (`suffixWhat()`), MISSING_LOCANTS for a suffix locant.
- **ESTER_IN_CHAIN** (new, BRANCH_AT_END's twin, `branchAtEnd()`): beside an acid, an alkoxycarbonyl prefix on an end of the
  student's chain whose C=O carbon the engine's parent takes in; step `groupChain`, whose sentence says «El carbono del éster que se
  nombra con «4-metoxi» y «4-oxo» sí forma parte de la cadena».
- **How the –COO– is written** (`DIAGNOSIS_PHRASES.ending()`, `principalEnding()`): «con la terminación «-oato» y, después de «de»,
  el grupo unido al otro oxígeno, acabado en «-ilo»» (`-dioato` / `-dicarboxilato` with «los grupos unidos a los otros oxígenos,
  acabados en «-ilo»»), or «con «benzoato» (el benceno con un grupo –COO–) …».

## Decisions

- **Codes: the C=O groups' where the message holds, new where it must differ** (as I-51d3 did for the acid). ESTER joins
  CARBONYL_GROUPS, so every shared code and phrase takes `group` 'ester'; three new codes: ESTER_GROUP (no message says that a C=O and
  an alkoxy oxygen on one carbon are one group), ESTER_CARBON (ACID_CARBON is named after the acid) and ESTER_IN_CHAIN (BRANCH_AT_END
  says nothing of how the ester is then written).
- **Order** `ESTER_GROUP → CARBONYL_SENIORITY → KETONE_NOT_ALDEHYDE → ESTER_CARBON → CARBONYL_PREFIX → principalParent → rules`, the
  acid's; esterChecks() and acidChecks() are exclusive by the engine's principal group, and DIAGNOSES puts ESTER_GROUP after
  ACID_GROUP, ESTER_CARBON after ACID_CARBON, ESTER_IN_CHAIN after BRANCH_AT_END.
- **ESTER_GROUP reads the engine's suffix –COO– groups only:** beside an acid the engine itself writes an ester whose C=O carbon is a
  chain carbon as `oxo` and an `-oxi` prefix, and an ester prefix written as a substituted methyl is a `words` null (R-6a3b).
- **CARBONYL_PREFIX: an alkoxycarbonyl at the parent level only, an acyloxy at any depth.** A nested alkoxycarbonyl is the parent
  choice, as a nested `carboxi` (`[(metoxicarbonil)metil]ciclohexano` → CARBONYL_PARENT, true: the ring is a branch of the acid
  part); a nested acyloxy would get CARBONYL_PARENT's «el anillo va en una rama», false (the ring goes into the O-bound group). The
  ester variant puts the long ending last: «no se nombra con el prefijo «…», sino …».
- **Seniority phrase:** «ácido > éster > aldehído > cetona > alcohol» when the principal group or the student's junior suffix is an
  ester, as the explanation's ester group step (and the acid's beside an ester prefix) words it; an acid beside no ester keeps
  «ácido > aldehído > cetona > alcohol» (no earlier message changes).
- **The O-bound side is not compared when the student's suffix cites another number of –COO– groups** (esterSideDiffers()): the
  side then differs because of the count (`4-metoxi-4-oxobutanoato de metilo`, «de metilo» for «de dimetilo»), which is the
  mistake; esterCategory() already ranks `count` before `oBound`.
- **The O-bound groups' own branches are read** (oBoundOccurrences()) for the student's –COOH and –COO– groups: without it, once
  esterChecks() stopped answering null for an ester's suffix beside an acid, `etanoato de 2-carboxietilo` would have got ACID_GROUP
  (the `carboxi` written in the O-bound group taken for a split –COOH); it now gets CARBONYL_SENIORITY «…con el prefijo
  «acetiloxi»».
- **ESTER_IN_CHAIN only beside an acid and when the C=O carbon is the engine's parent's**; trace rule 'P1', as BRANCH_AT_END.
- **Chemistry:** nothing from memory; the seniority is naming/seniority.js's, every name and form the forward engine's.

  random names gave no ETHER_SIDE across a bridge oxygen.
