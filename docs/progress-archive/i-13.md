# I-13 — Visible carbon dots in skeletal mode

Spec: `autoclaude/processed/130-visible-carbons.md`. Risk: routine. Worker: opus.

## Acceptance

- Every carbon (chain ends, bends, methane) has a filled dot in Esqueleto mode,
  e2e-verified (dot count = atom count, each dot visible and uncovered) for a
  nearly collinear 4-carbon chain, methane, after "Ordenar dibujo" with
  highlight, hover and selection, and a Cadena drag — met.
- Con carbonos shows no dots — met (e2e).
- Existing hover/highlight e2e still pass — met.

## Implementation

- `src/editor/render.js`: dots drawn by the renderer above the hover,
  selection and highlight layers, `pointer-events: none`, 3.5 units across
  (bonds are 2), scaling with zoom. Pure helpers `showsCarbonDots()` and
  `atomLabelPosition()`. Drag previews (Cadena, bond drag) show accent dots.
- Methane's `CH₄` label moves below its dot in Esqueleto (worker's choice).
- `src/editor/geometry.js`: `hitTest()` treats a box around a lone carbon's
  label as that carbon (`LONE_LABEL_OFFSET`, `onLoneLabel()`), so clicking the
  label acts on methane in both modes. Side effect: a bond drag snapping onto
  that label area joins the carbon.
- `docs/design.md` §6.3 rewritten; Ayuda sentence in `index.html` mentions the
  dots.

## Review

Codex `ship-with-fixes` — `docs/reviews/I-13.md`. One should-fix: the moved
CH₄ label fell outside the atom's click target. Fixed in `geometry.js` with
unit (`tests/unit/editor-geometry.test.js`) and e2e
(`tests/e2e/carbon-dots.spec.js`) regressions.
