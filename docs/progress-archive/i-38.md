## Acceptance

- `etanonitrilo`, `propanonitrilo`, `2-metilpropanonitrilo`, `butanodinitrilo` named; the C≡N is never an
  alkyne (no `-ino`, no triple bond in the parent) and its N never an amine; branched nitriles take the
  chain that ends at the nitrile carbon (`2-butiloctanonitrilo`, `3-pentilpentanodinitrilo`) — met
  (`tests/unit/nitriles.test.js`, fixtures).
- Ring nitriles and 3+ nitriles refused with specific Spanish messages (`ringNitrile`, `manyNitriles`);
  `ciano-` refused specifically (`cyanoPrefix`) — met.

## Implementation

- Validation (`src/model/validate.js`): `isNitrileCarbon()` (one lone N on a triple bond, at most one
  carbon neighbour on a single bond), `isNitrileNitrogen()`, `nitrileCarbons()`; the nitrile N is
  nameable in `hasNameableHeteroatoms()`; `nitrilePlacementError()` inside `oxygenPlacementError()` (after
  the amides); new messages `RING_NITRILE_MESSAGE`, `MANY_NITRILES_MESSAGE`, `CYANO_PREFIX_MESSAGE`; the
  generic HETEROATOM message now lists nitriles.
- Engine: `principal.js` kind 'nitrile' for the N (`groupKindOf()`, `NAMED_KINDS` after the amide), the N
  is the suffix site like an amine's N; `render.js` adds 'nitrile' to `TERMINAL_SUFFIXES`;
  `carbonylTraditionalId()` gives 'acetonitrile' for the bare etanonitrilo; `index.js` safety net
  `cyanoPrefix` when a nitrile is left out of the suffix, and `amineClassName()` only for an amine N (a
  lone nitrile N used to reach `nameSubstituent()` with a triple bond).
- `parent.js assertNoTripleBondLeaves()` (the v1 invariant "no triple bond leaves a longest chain",
  the "old assertion" of the spec) now checks carbon neighbours only: the C≡N leaves the chain by design.
  Kept for C≡C, where it still holds, rather than deleted.
- Lexicons: `acetonitrilo` (es) / `acetonitrile` (en), labelled as retained PIN.
- Explanation (`src/explain/explain.js`): `isNitrile()`, SUFFIX_GROUP_WORDS `nitrile`,
  `nitrileGroupStep()`, `allNitrogenSentences()` (nitrile N drawn N, before any amine N;
  `nitrogenDrawingSentences()` takes the other nitrogens into account), `atomCounts()` (one N, two π per
  –C≡N), `oxygenGroups()` (a nitrile is no C=O), chain and assemble sentences, prefix descriptions
  («el nitrilo va antes que…»), and the refusal groups step notes the C≡N is no alkyne.
- Oracle: `nitrilate()` / `generateNitriles()` in `scripts/oracle/generate.mjs`, wired into `run.mjs`
  (`acetonitrile` checked too).
- Help text (`index.html`), README, CLAUDE.md scope sentence, design.md (§4 render/omission/traditional,
  validation list, HETEROATOM table row, §5, §8 oracle, ring table, §13.6, rows I-38, I-39, I-40).

## Decisions

- `metanonitrilo` (HC≡N) is admitted: the engine handles a one-carbon chain with the N as its suffix
  site cleanly (like `metanamida`); no chain step, the group step says its carbon is the only one.
- `ciano-` is refused (`cyanoPrefix`), left to I-39: the prefix takes the nitrile carbon out of the
  skeleton, and parent selection, branch chains and the explanation's carbon counts all treat every
  carbon as a skeleton carbon. The same reason covers two nitriles on different carbon pieces (joined
  through an O or an N, one would be `ciano-` inside a branch), even where the ether/amine is symmetric.
- Dinitriles are named like diacids (`butanodinitrilo`, `etanodinitrilo`); a third nitrile is
  `manyNitriles`.
- Any nitrile with a ring is `ringNitrile` (I-40), including a ring on a nitrile's chain.
- Cl–C≡N, cyanates (O–C≡N) and cyanamides (N–C≡N) keep the generic HETEROATOM message.
- The e2e "not nameable yet" drawing in `elements.spec.js` and the generic-refusal example in
  `amines.spec.js` switched from a nitrile to an imine (C=N), still refused.

## Decisions (from memory, not checked online)

- Elision: IUPAC 2013 P-16.7.1 elides the final «e» (Spanish «o») of the parent only before a suffix
  starting with a vowel, so it is kept before `-nitrilo`: `etanonitrilo`, `prop-2-enonitrilo`,
  `butanodinitrilo`; English `prop-2-enenitrile`, `butanedinitrile`. The existing `suffixWords()` rule
  already does this; OPSIN accepts every English form.
- IUPAC 2013 retains acetonitrile as the preferred name (P-66.5.1.1.1); offered under "Otras formas
  válidas" only, for the bare molecule. Not offered: `formonitrilo` / `cianuro de hidrógeno` for HC≡N
  (status uncertain), `propionitrilo`, `acrilonitrilo`, and any `cianuro de …` name (functional-class
  names of nitriles are not IUPAC 2013 names).
- Seniority amide > nitrile > aldehyde (P-41).
