# I-50c — v4 R-4c: retained templates and the orto/meta/para letters in «¿Cómo se dibuja?»

- **Spec:** `docs/design.md` §15.4–§15.9, §14.4 (o/m/p rules) and §14.5 (retained names), §15.8 "R-4"
  (last third, first part: `tolueno`, `estireno`, `xileno`, `mesitileno`, the o/m/p aliases, the
  Ayuda line, e2e) — read from the working tree (the user's uncommitted edits). The ring diagnosis
  messages were split out into I-50d (`diagnose()` still returns `null` for every ring verdict).
- **Risk / worker:** high / opus (implementation worker).

## What was built

- Tiles (`src/reverse/tiles.js`): a new «Nombres tradicionales» row (row id `traditional`, design.md
  §15.2) with the four templates (kind `template`; texts from the lexicon's `TRADITIONAL_NAMES`,
  `xileno` being the word of `1,2-xileno`), described by `TEMPLATES` (defining group, count,
  positions — `null` for `xileno`, whose positions the student gives) and `templateGroupWord()`
  (`metilo`, `etenilo`, lexicon `groupName()`); the «Anillos» row gains the letters `o`, `m`, `p`
  (kind `positional`, value 2 / 3 / 4, the `POSITIONAL_LETTERS` keys; ids `POSITIONAL_IDS`).
- Parser (`parse.js`): `prefix = [locants] [letter] [MULT] substituent`, `parent = … | [locants |
  letter] template`; a `letter` field on prefix and parent nodes, a `template` node; the parent's
  `legacy` holds `xileno`'s locants. New errors: `TEMPLATE_LOCANTS` (numbers or a letter on
  `tolueno` / `estireno` / `mesitileno`), `POSITIONAL` (`checkLetters()`, design.md §14.4); also
  `LOCANT_COUNT` for `xileno` with other than two numbers, `TILE_ORDER` for a multiplier before a
  template or a template inside a compound branch.
- Assembler / tokenizer: a letter is written and punctuated as a locant (`o-xileno`,
  `o-dimetilbenceno`, `o-di-tert-butilbenceno`, `o-bis(2-metilpropil)benceno`), a template word as
  the parent's stem; a letter counts as a locant in the crowded case. Tokenizer classes `TPL`
  (ends a name, after prefixes, locants or a letter) and `POS` (only at the start).
- `interpret.js`: a template is a benzene ParentSpec (kind `benzene`) with `template` — a
  TemplateSpec `{value, word, groups}`, its groups as PrefixSpecs: implicit slots for the fixed
  positions, supplied ones from `xileno`'s numbers or letter, two left-out slots on the template
  tile for a bare `xileno`; `traditional` set; `oldStyle` only for chain parents. A letter gives
  supplied slots (`letter: true`): 1 and its locant, less those a template's group takes
  (`p-metiltolueno` → 4).
- `build.js`: `templateGroups()`; the groups are attached before the student's prefixes and counted
  in `specCarbons()` / `specRings()` / `checkSpec()`. A group a template places by rule is the
  ring's own (no bond order to blame), so a branch on its carbon is the VALENCE culprit with the
  reason; ranges worded «El anillo del xileno tiene 6 carbonos…».
- `search.js`: the template's groups are in the search model (the student's prefixes are searched on
  the free carbons, numbered from the template's group); a bare `xileno`'s methyls are one group of
  left-out slots like `dimetilbenceno`'s; `filledTree()` writes them before the template
  (`1,2-xileno`).
- Verdicts: every form the engine gives is `alternative` with the engine's own label — `tolueno`,
  `estireno`, `mesitileno`, `1,2-` / `1,3-` / `1,4-xileno`, `o-` / `m-` / `p-xileno` (traditional
  label), `o-` / `m-` / `p-dimetilbenceno`, `p-dietenilbenceno`, `o-diisopropilbenceno`,
  `o-di-tert-butilbenceno`, `o-bis(2-metilpropil)benceno` (orto/meta/para label). `xileno` →
  `ambiguous` «Faltan números: puede ser 1,2-xileno, 1,3-xileno o 1,4-xileno.»; `4-metiltolueno`,
  `p-metiltolueno`, `4-metil-1,2-xileno`, `1,6-xileno`, `pentametiltolueno` → `otherName` with the
  engine's name; `metiltolueno`, `etiltolueno`, `metilxileno` → `ambiguous`; `1-metiltolueno`,
  `7-metiltolueno`, `1,7-xileno` → `invalid`; `feniltolueno` → `outOfScope`.
- Dialog (`src/ui/name-builder.js`, `css/app.css`): the new row; each letter tile shows its word
  small beside it (`nb-tile-hint`) and is named «p (para, 1,4)» (`positionalLabel()`); colours for
  the new kinds.
- Ayuda (`index.html`): the «Del nombre al dibujo» scope sentence now says «o con un anillo» (it
  still said open chains only since R-2b), and a new paragraph `#help-name-to-drawing-rings`
  covers `ciclo`, `benceno` / `fenil`, the templates and the letters.
- `CLAUDE.md` v4 sentence updated (R-4c done; next R-4d / I-50d, ring diagnosis messages).
- Tests: new `tests/unit/reverse-templates.test.js` (16 tests: parse, errors with tile ranges,
  assemble, interpret, build, search, every alternative, other names, left-out locants, refused
  forms, the ordered drawing, a seeded no-crash property, time budget) and
  `tests/e2e/name-builder-templates.spec.js` (3 tests × 2 projects); updated
  `reverse-{tiles,parse,build,tokenize,benzene}.test.js` (catalogue, error codes, NameSpec shape,
  the R-4c tokenize examples and a property test with templates and letters) and the helper
  `reverse-words.js` (`isBenzeneName()` now accepts the template and o/m/p forms, `isTemplateName()`,
  the fixture `section` of each row, letters in `leafIndices()`).

## Decisions and deviations (worker)

- Row: the templates go in a new «Nombres tradicionales» row, the one design.md §15.2 plans for
  `tolueno`, `acetona`, `ácido acético`… (§15.1), rather than «Anillos»; the letters, which only
  number a benzene ring, go in «Anillos».
- A template is a ParentSpec of kind `benzene` with a `template` field, not a kind of its own as
  §15.4 sketches: every benzene rule of R-4b (aromatic key, ring carbons 1…6, no bond slots, the
  ring limit, `diagnose()` returning null) applies unchanged.
- Substituted templates are read, not refused: the engine never writes them (§14.5, «never a
  substituted tolueno/xileno»), so a student's `4-metiltolueno` describes its molecule and gets
  `otherName` («1,4-dimetilbenceno»), or `ambiguous` with left-out locants — the search handles the
  template's ring positions relative to its group.
- o/m/p letters (§14.4): accepted only on a name-level prefix or on `xileno`, with a benzene or
  template parent and exactly two occupied ring positions, a template's group counting as one
  (`o-dimetilbenceno`, `o-xileno`, and `p-metiltolueno` read as the methyl at 4 → `otherName`; the
  same rule will read `p-metilfenol` in R-5a). Refused as `invalid` (`POSITIONAL`, the letter
  marked): three or more positions (`o-trimetilbenceno`, `o-dimetiltolueno`, `4-metil-o-xileno`),
  one position (`o-metilbenceno`), two different prefixes on `benceno` (`m-etilmetilbenceno`),
  numbers and a letter together, another parent (`o-hexano`), before `benceno`, and inside a
  compound branch (`(p-metilfenil)`: the engine never writes a letter on a phenyl, §14.4 / I-45a).
  `p-xileno` stays a retained name (traditional label), apart from `p-dimetilbenceno` (orto/meta/para
  label) — both labels come from the engine's alternatives.
- The I-50b archive counted the skipped alternatives as «six xylene forms, five o/m/p forms»; the 18
  are `tolueno` ×2, `estireno`, eight xylene forms (four rows, the 1,2 row twice), `mesitileno` and
  six o/m/p forms (`o-dimetilbenceno` ×2, `m-` / `p-dimetilbenceno`, `p-dietenilbenceno`,
  `o-diisopropilbenceno`). All 18 now run in both fixture round trips (25 rows, 49 names, none
  skipped); the 10 I-42a rows with halogen or alkoxy prefixes are asserted as the only ones left
  out (R-5a / R-5b).
- The «src/reverse never retypes a tile word» test skips the one-letter tile words: code uses such
  letters for other things (the conjunction «o» of `index.js` `orBefore()`); the catalogue test
  checks that `o`, `m`, `p` come from `POSITIONAL_LETTERS`.
- No file in `src/naming/` changed; `diagnose.js` only its header (I-50d named for the ring
  messages).

  «p»: escribe los números, como en (4-metilfenil).»
