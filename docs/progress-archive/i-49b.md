## What was built

- `showVerdict()` (`src/ui/results.js`): a diagnosed `otherName` verdict puts `diagnosis.message`
  between «…pero se llama «X».» and «Pulsa «Ver paso a paso» para ver por qué.»; a `null`
  diagnosis and every other verdict keep their text. The verdict block gets a legend
  («Cadena principal» / «Tu cadena», or «Tu cadena (es la misma)» when both chains coincide) and
  a «Ver el paso» button that opens the stepper at `diagnosisExplanation(...).index` and moves the
  focus to the step title.
- Canvas: a new `student` highlight style (`src/editor/render.js`, `css/app.css`) — a wider striped
  band with dashed rings drawn under the solid parent band; `withStudentChain()` (pure, exported
  from `results.js`) composes it into every non-option view. Works in the 90° view; the highlight
  switch («Resaltar en el dibujo», `organicWeb.highlights`) hides chain and legend together.
- Clearing: Deshacer of the load and any chemical edit clear text, legend and chain; a
  coordinate-only edit keeps them.
- `src/ui/draw-name.js`: verdict block entries (diagnosis line, style line, legend entries).
  `src/reverse/index.js`: `VERDICT_TEXTS.oldStyle` (the old-style note text, unchanged).
- Ayuda line in «Del nombre al dibujo» (`index.html`); `CLAUDE.md` v4 sentence now says the panel
  shows the diagnosis and that R-4 (I-50) is next.
- Tests: `tests/unit/highlights.test.js`, `tests/unit/draw-name.test.js` (extended);
  `tests/e2e/diagnosis.spec.js` (new: two diagnosis codes, highlight + legend, «Ver el paso»
  step, Deshacer, keyboard-only, narrow viewport, 90° view, coordinate-only edit, verdicts without
  a diagnosis); `tests/e2e/name-builder.spec.js` (`metiletano` / `2-buteno` texts updated by
  design).

## Decisions

- **Student's prefix style.** For a diagnosed verdict the whole stepper (both «Ver paso a paso» and
  «Ver el paso») explains the naming in the student's prefix style (`diagnosisExplanation()`);
  «Se llama» and «Otras formas válidas» keep the app's default name. When the explained name
  differs from it, one line before the diagnosis gives that name with its label and says the
  stepper uses it. Consequence: with the stepper closed, the ordered drawing's locants follow the
  explained naming (`6-metil-4-(propan-2-il)nonano` is numbered as `4-metil-6-(propan-2-il)nonano`
  while «Se llama» shows `4-isopropil-6-metilnonano`), so the canvas never backs the student's
  numbering. Documented in the headers of `draw-name.js` and `results.js`. Open to the user's
  preference (see *Open risks* in `PROGRESS.md`).
- **OLD_STYLE.** An `OLD_STYLE` diagnosis replaces the old-style note (it says the same and more);
  beside any other diagnosis the note stays.

## Review fix (coordinator)

- Codex should-fix (`results.js` ~470): after a coordinate-only edit the drawing is no longer the
  ordered layout, so `withOrderedMarks()` stopped adding the parent band while the student band
  and the two-chain legend stayed. Fix: `withStudentChain(view, chain, isOption, parent)` also adds
  the diagnosis's parent (`diagnosis.parent`, kept as `parentChain` beside `studentChain`, reset
  with it) when the view has no parent band of its own. Unit assertions in `highlights.test.js`;
  the e2e coordinate-only test now checks the parent band after the move and after its undo.
