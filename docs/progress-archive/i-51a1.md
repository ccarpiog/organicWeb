## What was built

- **Tiles:** kind `halogen`, value the element symbol (`F`, `Cl`, `Br`, `I`), texts from the lexicon's `HALOGEN_PREFIXES`,
  ids `halogen-F`… (`HALOGEN_IDS`), in a new row «Grupos» (`groups`) between «Anillos» and «Nombres tradicionales» (the
  order design.md §15.2 lists); I-51a2 adds `ol` / `hidroxi` there.
- **Parser:** `substituent = simple | halogen | START … END`; a `{type: 'halogen', tile}` node, complete by itself, never a
  compound branch's own chain (`(2-cloro)` → TILE_ORDER at its END: «antes de cerrar la rama compuesta va su propia
  cadena…»). `checkLetters()` unchanged: it already reads `p-diclorobenceno` (accepted), `o-bromoclorobenceno` (two
  different prefixes, POSITIONAL), `p-diclorotolueno` (three positions, POSITIONAL), `m-clorotolueno` (accepted, a
  substituted template).
- **Assembler / tokenizer:** a halogen is a simple prefix piece (part kind `prefix`), never enclosed, also crowded
  (render.js encloses only alkoxy and ring prefixes there: `bromoclorofluoroyodometano`, `(clorofluorometil)`,
  `cloro(fenil)metano`). Tokenizer class `HAL` wherever a retained word may stand.
- **Interpreter / builder:** SubstituentSpec kind `halogen` (no chain, no free-valence slot, `element`); one atom bonded by
  a single bond, adding one bond to its carbon (VALENCE `1,1,1,1-tetracloroetano` on the fourth locant; `pentaclorometano`
  «No caben…»; `hexaclorotolueno`). The heavy-atom cap (model/validate.js MAX_HEAVY_ATOMS, 80, with the engine's
  `TOO_MANY_ATOMS_MESSAGE`) is now checked on the NameSpec after the carbon cap: halogens count for it, never for the carbon
  cap or the chains. `ownPrefixAtoms()` cuts a compound branch's atoms by atoms created (`substituentAtoms()`), not carbons.
- **Search:** halogens are carbon slots of the chain they hang from (`hasOwnChain()` keeps them out of the chain models),
  pruned by the carbons' free valence: `dodecafluorociclohexano` is one placement, `hexaclorobuta-1,3-dieno` one placement,
  `dicloroetano` three placements in two structures.
- **Verdicts:** every §15.5.3 halogen case and the fixture/generated names as above; `1-(4-clorofenil)propano`,
  `4-clorotolueno`, `p-clorotolueno`, `2-cloroestireno` → `otherName` with their ring diagnosis; `p-diclorobenceno` →
  `alternative` (the engine's positional label).
- **Diagnosis (`diagnose.js`):** rule 1 skips halogens (a halogen on an end carbon never lengthens the chain:
  `1-cloro-1-metilpropano` blames `metil`); the style shapes know halogens (`X:Cl`, the same in every style); UNGROUPED of
  halogens says «Los sustituyentes iguales…» and MORE_SUBSTITUENTS in a molecule with halogens «…gana la que tiene más
  sustituyentes» (the hydrocarbon wording unchanged); NEEDLESS_LOCANTS also covers the prefix locants of a one-carbon
  compound branch (`(1-clorometil)`, `(1,1-diclorometan-1-il)` → `(diclorometil)`), which only halogens make possible.
  Chain rules 2–4 (P4, N3, N4) need nothing: the engine's trace counts the halogens. Rule 9 on one- and two-carbon parents
  and full halogenation (`1-clorometano`, `1-cloroetano`, `1,1,1,2,2,2-hexacloroetano`) works through the existing
  `parentOmissions()`.
- **Dialog / Ayuda:** the row and its tile colour; `#help-name-to-drawing` now says «… o con un anillo, y de sus derivados
  halogenados», and a new `#help-name-to-drawing-groups` paragraph covers the «Grupos» row.

## Decisions

- A halogen is a node and spec kind of its own (`halogen`), not a `retained` word: retained words are carbon groups with
  templates (isopropil, tert-butil) and the code that handles them (atoms, attachment order, chain models) would have
  needed a check per use either way.
- **Deliberate, tested nulls:** a compound branch numbered from its far end (`5-(1-cloroetan-2-il)nonano`,
  `5-(1-cloropropan-3-il)nonano`) — no rule covers a branch's own numbering (diagnose.js «No rule covers»). Before this
  phase `shortForm()` would have dropped such a free-valence locant and told the student to write `(1-cloroetil)`, a
  different molecule; it now keeps a free-valence locant other than 1 when the branch has prefixes of its own.
- **Affects chains too:** the same fix changes one hydrocarbon behaviour: `5-(1-metiletan-2-il)nonano` (a propyl branch
  numbered from its far end) got NEEDLESS_LOCANTS «se escribe «5-(1-metiletil)»» (isopropyl, wrong); it is now null.
  `5-(1-metiletan-1-il)nonano` keeps its NEEDLESS_LOCANTS. No existing test changed; both are regressions in
  `reverse-halogens.test.js`.
- `triacontafluorotriacontano` (30 fluorines over 30 carbons, left out) exceeds the search budget: `incomplete`, never a
  claim (design.md §15.5.3 step 3); with 30 more chlorines it is TOO_BIG before any search.
- The POSITIONAL «different» message keeps its fixed lexicon example (`1-etil-2-metilbenceno`) for
  `o-bromoclorobenceno`, as for any two different prefixes.

## Deviations

- None from the brief's requirements. Beyond them: a tokenize property test with halogen prefixes
  (`reverse-tokenize.test.js`), a seeded `readName()` no-crash property, and the `shortForm()` fix above.
- `reverse-tokenize.test.js` listed `clorometano` among texts that do not tokenize; it is replaced by `cloro` and
  `(2-cloro)etano`, which only read with errors.
