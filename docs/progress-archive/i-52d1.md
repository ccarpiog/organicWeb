## What was built

- **Tiles:** «Grupos» gains `amida` and `carboxamida` after `amino`; «Anillos» gains `benzamida` after `benzonitrilo`. Every
  text from the lexicon. SUFFIX_GROUPS `amide` (terminal, like `oico`) and `carboxamide` (outside, like `carboxílico`), both
  `order` 2 with `amideNitrogen` (the group's carbon also carries the nitrogen, single-bonded, as an acid's carries its OH);
  GROUP_WORD_PARENTS `benzamide` (its group at carbon 1, «grupo amida», feminine).
- **Grammar:** `suffix = … | amida | carboxamida`; `parent = … | benzamida`; `locant = … | N` now also on a name-level prefix
  of an amide. `amida` mirrors `nitrilo` (no locants, TILE_ORDER after a ring or `benceno`, old-style locants the segment's);
  `carboxamida` takes ring locants like `carbonitrilo`; `benzamida` is `benzonitrilo`'s twin (TEMPLATE_LOCANTS, an o/m/p letter
  counting its group). checkNitrogen() keeps `N` to an amine's or an amide's name-level prefixes. No new error code.
- **Assembler / tokenizer:** nothing of their own: `amida` starts with a vowel (`etanamida`, `prop-2-enamida`), `diamida` and
  `carboxamida` with a consonant (`etanodiamida`, `ciclohexanocarboxamida`), as render.js suffixWords() does; the tokenizer reads
  the suffixes as OL and `benzamida` as PHOL.
- **Interpreter / builder:** SuffixSpec `amideNitrogen`; nitrogenSlots() gives an amide's prefixes the `N` slots an amine's get
  (domain `[N_VALUE, n]`, left-out ones only on a one-group amide and for the carbon branches the engine puts on an N). The
  builder puts the =O and then the nitrogen on the group's carbon (the chain's end, or a carbon outside the ring), the first
  group's nitrogen taking the `N` prefixes; VALENCE on a third group; CARBONYL_POSITION for more amide groups than the chain's
  ends; polyamideRefusal() refuses an `N` beside two or more amide groups before any placement.
- **Search:** no new role; the amide's nitrogen counts its bonds at index N_VALUE (two free); `carboxamida`'s suffix list may be
  kept without locants beside an `N` (the engine omits it, `N-metilciclohexanocarboxamida`).
- **Diagnosis (`diagnose.js`, header «Amides»):** outsideRules() lets amides through; amideChecks() runs right after
  amineChecks() and answers an AMIDE_NULLS draft for the amide's own mistakes; the guards give `parent` (principalParent(), P0)
  and `numbering` (N0) and `suffix` (the suffix's needless or missing locants) nulls. Every other rule runs and was checked to
  say something true of an amide name (sweeps above, every step resolving).
- **Dialog:** the three tiles with tooltips; the `N` tooltip says the amides use it too.

## Decisions

- **The amide's nitrogen is a suffix field (`amideNitrogen`), like an acid's OH (`hydroxy`) and an ester's bridge oxygen
  (`bridge`)**, not an element of its own: the group's atom (`attachment`) stays its =O, as the engine's SuffixLocant
  `attachAtom`, and the nitrogen is `amideNitrogen` / `amideBond`, the engine's own field names.
- **`N` is reused as is:** the locant value N_VALUE, the domain widening (amineSlots() renamed nitrogenSlots()) and the builder's
  `state.nitrogen` serve both families through `isNitrogenSuffix()`; the engine gives both the same N_LOCANT and the same
  omission rule (render.js carbonLocantPrefixes(), `omitGroupLocants`), so the search's filled names and fill variants needed
  only the kind test widened.
- **polyamideRefusal() apart from polyamineRefusal():** the engine's messages differ, and the I-52c1 review's reason holds for
  amides too (beside a senior group the builder would otherwise put the group on a nitrogen of its own choosing).
- **`carboxamida` after a chain is read and judged, not a parse error**, as I-52b1 decided for `carbonitrilo`
  (`propano-1-carboxamida` → `otherName` «butanamida», a `carbon` null for I-52d4).
- **Ranked nulls (AMIDE_NULLS), as the amines' of I-52c1, not BEYOND:** a `carboxamida` with its locant left out is read in
  every placement, and a placement numbering the amide otherwise (an N0 null of rule 4) must not null the kinder reading's rule
  (`N-metil-N-metilciclohexanocarboxamida` → UNGROUPED).
- **amideChecks() right after amineChecks():** a student's `amina` beside an amide keeps the amine's worded CARBONYL_SENIORITY
  (`N-acetiletanamina` → «N-etiletanamida», R-6c4a); a student's amide suffix beside an acid or an ester must not reach their
  seniority code, whose words would name the junior group as a separate one.
- **«grupo amida»** (AMIDE_GROUP) in the parser's and builder's messages, not «grupo CONH₂»: the group may be –CONH–, –CON<
  (model/validate.js says «grupo amida» too); «la benzamida» with its article (withArticle()).
- **An `N` alone before the root of a terminal suffix** (`N et ano amida`) now gets checkNitrogen()'s hint (««N» va delante de
  una rama unida al nitrógeno de la amida…») instead of ««amida» no lleva números», which would call `N` a number; numbers there
  keep the old hint.
- **No `carbamoil` word in the new hints:** the branch form of an amide is I-52d2's tile, so `benzamideBranch` and the suffix
  hints say only where the word goes (I-52d2 should add the `carbamoil` clause, as `cianofenil` for nitriles).
- **Tests:** the R-6d1 names that joined the amine, amino, nitrile and acyl round trips are swept in the amide file
  (`isR6d1AmideName()`), so every earlier sweep keeps its names and exact counts (the I-52a2b / I-52b1 precedent).
- **Chemistry:** nothing from memory; every name, form and refusal is the forward engine's.

## Open questions / deviations

- The sub-phase split follows the brief (d1 suffixes and `N`, d2 `carbamoil` and acylamino, d3 retained names and Ayuda, d4
  messages), not the three-way split PROGRESS.md "Next action" sketched (which put `carbamoil` in d1); the phase table is the
  orchestrator's.
- Pre-existing, cross-family (not amide-specific; the nitrile, acid and hydrocarbon twins behave the same): a prefix in a style
  the engine never writes with the systematic benzene form (`N-(propan-2-il)bencenocarboxamida`,
  `4-(propan-2-il)benceno-1-carboxamida`) or mixing two styles (`N-tert-butil-N-(1-metiletil)prop-2-enamida`,
  `1-tert-butil-4-(1-metiletil)benceno`) gets a null; part of the open question «a retained word's prefix style».
- `ciclohexilmetanamida` (locant left out) is `missingLocants` «N-ciclohexilmetanamida» (N-cyclohexylformamide): of the
  structures, only the one with the ring on the nitrogen fills to an engine name (§15.5.3 step 6); `1-ciclohexilmetanamida` is
  the `carbon` mistake. Worth a teacher's look.
- Residual risk (as AMINE_SIDE's): amideChecks()'s `parent` category compares the student's =O atoms with the engine's
  through one isomorphism; a molecule with two symmetric amide groups on different pieces could map them crosswise. No case
  found: the symmetric ones are refused by the engine (`oxidi`, two halves across an O).
- design.md §15.8's Status column is not updated (the user's uncommitted file).
- No forward-engine bug found.
