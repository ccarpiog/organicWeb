## What was built

- **Tiles:** «Grupos» gains, at its end, `oato`, `carboxilato` (suffixes) and `ilo` (kind `esterAlkyl`); «Anillos» gains
  `benzoato` after `benzoico`. Every text from the lexicon. SUFFIX_GROUPS `ester` (terminal, like `oico`) and `carboxylate`
  (outside, like `carboxílico`), both with `bridge` (the bridge oxygen beside the =O); GROUP_WORD_PARENTS `benzoate` (its
  –COO– at carbon 1, «grupo COO»).
- **Grammar:** `name = [ácido], {prefix}, parent, [esterSide]`; `esterSide = esterGroup, {esterGroup}`; `esterGroup = {prefix},
  oBound | (MULT|CMULT), (oBound | START {prefix} oBound END)`; `oBound` a chain or ring with `ilo` in its free-valence place
  (with the locant before it: `propan-2-ilo`) or `isopropil` / `tert-butil` / `fenil` followed by `ilo`; `suffix = … | oato
  | carboxilato`; `parent = … | benzoato`. After a parent with an ester suffix (or with an `ilo` tile anywhere after it) every
  tile is the O-bound side, read as the items of a compound branch; `assemble.js esterGroupsOf()` splits it into groups (each
  ends at an item ending in `ilo`, with the bare prefixes before it). `oato` mirrors `oico`: no locants, old style goes to the
  segment, TILE_ORDER after a ring or `benceno`; `benzoato` is `benzoico`'s twin.
- **Assembler (rule 10):** ` de ` after the parent when something follows, ` y ` between groups (both 'punct' pieces with the
  space role), a group's bare prefixes as inside a compound branch, a single group never enclosed (a word of its own:
  `2-cloroetilo`, `2-(4-metilfenil)etilo`, `4-metilfenilo`), a multiplied one enclosed as a prefix by itself
  (`di(propan-2-ilo)`, `bis(2-cloroetilo)`, `dimetilo`, `diisopropilo`, `di-tert-butilo`); a retained word's `il` cut before
  `ilo` (`isoprop` + `ilo`). Rule 4 already elides `ano` before `oato` and keeps it before `dioato` / `carboxilato`. 63 forms
  are checked against the engine's own text and tokenized back.
- **Tokenizer:** EST (`oato`, `carboxilato`) and PHEST (`benzoato`) where OL / PHOL stand, then exactly ` de `; ILO after a
  root, an elided ending, a locant or a retained word without its `il`; ` y ` only after a complete group outside every mark
  (a flag set past ` de `); an automatic enclosure closes after ILO; a compound O-bound group may end the name.
- **Interpreter / builder:** NameSpec `ester` `{groups: [{multiplier, substituent, tiles}], tiles}`, each substituent the
  group's SubstituentSpec (its bare prefixes numbered on it) with `oBound`; SuffixSpec `bridge`. Build puts the =O and the
  bridge oxygen on the –COO–'s carbon (the chain end for `oato`, three bonds; a new carbon outside for `carboxilato` and
  `benzoato`), then bonds occurrence k of the O-bound groups (expanded by their multipliers, in the student's order) to the
  k-th bridge oxygen (`attachEsterGroups()`, verdict `esterAtoms`). The caps and the ring count include the O-bound groups and
  the bridge oxygens (`benzoato de fenilo`: two rings, keyed). CARBONYL_POSITION reused for `oato` («…grupos COO…»).
- **Search:** no new role; each O-bound group's own chain is a chain of the model (its left-out locants searched:
  `etanoato de cloroetilo`), its free valence one bond; the bridge oxygen never limits.
- **Diagnosis (`diagnose.js`, header «Esters»):** `outsideRules()` lets esters through; `esterSideDiffers()` (before every
  rule) and `esterChecks()` (first in `firstRule()`) answer null for the ester's own mistakes, and BEYOND guards keep the P0,
  N0, parent-choice and suffix-locant rules from speaking of OH groups. Every other rule runs on the acid part and was checked
  to say something true: BRANCH_AT_END, LONGER_CHAIN, MORE_SUBSTITUENTS, NUMBERING, RING_NUMBERING (N1–N4), BENZENE_RING,
  BRANCH_RING_NUMBERING, ACYL_NAME, ALPHABETICAL_ORDER, UNGROUPED, MULTIPLIER_KIND, LOCANT_ORDER, NEEDLESS_LOCANTS (inside a
  branch), LETTER_LOCANTS, OLD_STYLE («eno» o «ino»; «dicarboxilato»).
- **Dialog:** the four tiles with tooltips; `ilo` in the prefixes' colour (`.nb-tile--esterAlkyl`).

## Decisions

- **The O-bound side's ending is one tile, `ilo` (kind `esterAlkyl`), written in place of `il`.** It mirrors the acyl
  endings `oil` / `carbonil` (the free-valence place of a chain: `met ilo`, `prop ano 2 ilo`, so every chain path — locants,
  the automatic `an`, the elision of `ano` / `eno` before a vowel, the enclosure — is reused) and `oxi` after a retained word
  (whose `il` it replaces: `isopropil ilo` → `isopropilo`, `fenil ilo` → `fenilo`, so no new retained tiles). Rejected: a
  tile `o` after `il` (its text would clash with the orto letter `o`, and it is no meaningful piece for a student), and whole
  words `metilo`, `isopropilo`… (they would duplicate every root and retained word). The text is the lexicon's
  FREE_VALENCE_SUFFIXES[1] + ESTER_ALKYL_ENDING (`il` + `o`, as render.js esterAlkylName()).
- **` de ` and ` y ` are automatic**, as `ácido`'s space: the student picks the groups (and their own `di` / `bis`, kept as
  chosen); ` y ` stands between two groups, in the student's order (the diagnosis of the order is I-52a3's).
- **A single O-bound group's own prefixes are written bare before its chain** (`2 cloro et ilo` → `2-cloroetilo`): the engine
  writes it without marks (a word of its own), so a compound branch around it would have no visible marks for the tokenizer
  to read. With a multiplier the group must be a compound branch (`bis START 2 cloro et ilo END`): the multiplier's scope, and
  the marks render.js writes. A single group written as a compound branch is accepted and assembled without marks.
- **Two new parse codes, ESTER_GROUPS and ESTER_SUFFIX**, each with its message and tiles; the shape of a group (locants
  before it, bare prefixes before a multiplied group, `il` for `ilo`…) is TILE_ORDER with its own hint, and when a group is
  misshapen its count is not reported too (one message per mistake).
- **O-bound occurrence k goes to the k-th –COO– of the suffix** (slot order). A mixed diester on an acid part that differs
  seen from either end is the engine's `mixedDiester` whatever the assignment, so the verdict is `outOfScope` either way.
- **The retained node gets `ilo` only when the tile follows it** (`ilo?`), so every earlier tree keeps its shape and their
  deepEqual tests stand.
- **The diagnosis reads the acid part only** (the O-bound side is no prefix of the name; the prefix style is recognised from
  the acid part's branches). Any O-bound side that is none the engine writes, in any style, makes the diagnosis null, even
  beside an acid-part mistake: which is the main mistake is for the ester rules of I-52a3 to decide.
- **The acyl round trip's `laterFamily()`** now reads the amide words before ` de `: its 5 ester rows with an amide prefix
  (`2-(acetilamino)etanoato de metilo`…) are R-6d's, so its later counts moved from 29 ester / 31 amide rows to 21 / 36.

## Deliberate nulls («Esters», for I-52a3)

- **Categories** (read independently in the tests, `esterCategory()`): `seniority` — the student's suffix no –COO– beside the
  engine's, or the reverse (`1-metoxipropanal`, `1-metoxi-1-oxopropano`, `3-metoxi-3-oxopropan-1-ol`); `count` — another
  number of –COO– groups (`4-metoxi-4-oxobutanoato de metilo`); `carbon` — the –COO–'s carbon on the other side of the parent
  (`propano-1-carboxilato de metilo`, `ciclohexilmetanoato de metilo`, `fenilmetanoato de metilo`); `oBound` — the O-bound side
  none the engine writes (`etanoato de propan-1-ilo`, `de metan-1-ilo`, `propanodioato de metilo y etilo`, `butanodioato de
  metilo y metilo`, `de di(2-cloroetilo)`, `de bismetilo`, `de butan-3-ilo`, `de 6-metilfenilo`); `numbering` — the –COO–
  groups numbered otherwise (N0: `ciclohexano-1,5-dicarboxilato de dimetilo`, `ciclohexano-2-carboxilato de metilo`);
  `suffix` — the suffix's needless locants (`ciclohexano-1-carboxilato de metilo`, `benceno-1-carboxilato de metilo`).
  Guarded too, though no case was found: a chain dropped at P0 and the ring or chain chosen by the –COO– groups.
- **Counts:** fixture sweep 94 of 155 `otherName` (90 `numbering`, 4 `oBound`); generated sweep 407 of 1040 (384
  `numbering`, 23 `oBound`); the carbonyl fixture sweep's 8 (`1-metoxipropanal`-type, `seniority`) unchanged.
