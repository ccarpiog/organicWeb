## What was built

- **Tile:** «Grupos» gains `carbamoil` after `carboxamida`, a retained word (kind `retained`, like `amino`), its value the
  language-neutral `carbamoylGroup`, its text the lexicon's GROUP_PREFIXES.amide.
- **Composition:** as `amino` (I-52c2): `carbamoil` alone is the –CONH₂ (`3 carbamoil`); the groups on its nitrogen are the prefixes of
  a compound branch whose own chain is `carbamoil`, written before it without locants (`START met il carbamoil END` →
  `(metilcarbamoil)`, `START et il met il carbamoil END` → `[etil(metil)carbamoil]`, `bis START met il carbamoil END`). The acylamino
  prefixes need no tile: an acyl word or ending among the groups on an amino nitrogen (`START acetil amino END` → `(acetilamino)`,
  `START acetil met il amino END` → `[acetil(metil)amino]`, `START START 4 met il benzoil END amino END` →
  `[(4-metilbenzoil)amino]`).
- **Parser:** `simple = … | carbamoil`; checkNitrogenGroups() keeps the groups on an amino or carbamoyl nitrogen without locants or
  letters (each with its own hint) and no longer refuses an acyl group on an amino nitrogen; `oxi` after `carbamoil`, `carbamoil` in an
  ether's class name or as an O-bound group are TILE_ORDER with hints; a name ending in a carbamoyl prefix ends in «un grupo».
- **Assembler (rule 12):** a carbamoyl prefix's nitrogen is a rule-12 level (every group after the first enclosed, no crowded rule), as
  render.js writes it; enclosed and compound with groups (`(metilcarbamoil)`, `bis(…)`), simple bare (`dicarbamoil`).
- **Tokenizer:** nothing of its own (`carbamoil` is a RET word).
- **Interpreter / builder / search:** a carbamoyl prefix is the amino prefix's spec with its own kind (its `length` 1 the amide's
  nitrogen, which takes the groups' implicit slots); the builder creates the C=O carbon (the occurrence's attachment), its =O and the
  nitrogen, then the groups (three bonds on the nitrogen: VALENCE «…del grupo carbamoil…»); one carbon and three heavy atoms besides the
  groups for the caps; the search models the nitrogen as an amino prefix's, the carbon full. An acyl group on an amino nitrogen is
  built as any acyl prefix (its C=O carbon bonded to the nitrogen).
- **Diagnosis:** a carbamoyl prefix's style shape is the engine's carbamoyl group (`C1:1:1(N:(…),X:=O)`); its groups and an
  acylamino prefix's are aminoGroups()' lists (read first); «sustituyentes» beside `carbamoil`; it is no hetero group (its carbon may
  join a chain). The amide-prefix mistakes are AMIDE_NULLS drafts (below).
- **Dialog:** the `carbamoil` tooltip; `amino`'s tooltip names the acylamino form.

## Decisions

- **`carbamoil` modelled as `amino`** (one tile, the groups on its nitrogen a compound branch's prefixes; one spec builder,
  nitrogenGroupSubstituent()), because render.js writes both the same way (carbamoylAmino()); no `metilcarbamoil`-style tiles, which
  would not compose with substituted groups (`[(2-cloroetil)carbamoil]`).
- **The spec's one atom is the nitrogen**, the C=O carbon and its =O the builder's (carbamoylGroup()): the search then needs no new
  role (the nitrogen is the amino prefix's one-atom chain of valence three; the carbon, full, is never a slot).
- **An acyl group on a carbamoyl nitrogen is built and refused by the engine** (an imide, its own message, `outOfScope`), as
  `N-acetiletanamida` was in I-52d1, rather than a new TILE_ORDER hint; two acyl groups on an amino nitrogen likewise.
- **`carbamoil` is no hetero group for rule 1:** its carbon may lengthen a chain. On an end of the student's chain whose carbon the
  engine's parent takes (`ácido 3-carbamoilpropanoico` → «ácido 4-amino-4-oxobutanoico») that is the amide's own mistake, the
  `inChain` null (ESTER_IN_CHAIN's twin; the esters' was an «Esters» null too before I-52a3a); off an end the trace's chain rules speak
  and are true (`ácido 3-carbamoilbutanoico` → MORE_SUBSTITUENTS «…con más sustituyentes (3; la tuya tiene 1)…»: `amino`, `metil`,
  `oxo`).
- **Inside a compound branch** a carbamoyl prefix the engine's name of that branch takes into its chain (`(carbamoilmetil)` →
  `(2-amino-2-oxoetil)`) is `inChain` too (branchNames(), carbamoylTakenIn()), and a branch the engine names `carbamoil` written as
  another kind (`(aminooxometil)`, `(1-amino-1-oxometil)`) is the `words` null (carbamoylWritten()), before NEEDLESS_LOCANTS could
  recommend «(aminooxometil)».
- **Shared rules on the nitrogen's groups** (ALPHABETICAL_ORDER, UNGROUPED, MULTIPLIER_KIND) speak on a carbamoyl or acylamino
  prefix, as on an amino prefix (I-52c2's decision); the acyl group of an acylamino prefix is judged by the branch-name rules (ACYL_NAME,
  ACYL_CHAIN, BRANCH_RING_NUMBERING), whose messages hold.
- **Hint examples:** the `amida` suffix hint names `(4-carbamoilciclohexil)`, an engine form (`ácido 2-(4-carbamoilciclohexil)etanoico`):
  on a chain branch the engine takes an amide's carbon into the branch's chain, so there is no `(carbamoilmetil)` to cite (the nitrile
  hint's `(cianometil)` has no twin). `benzamideBranch` first followed the I-52d1 decision and the `cianofenil` precedent,
  `(4-carbamoilfenil)`; the review fix replaced it (below, «Review fixes»).
- **Tests:** the R-6d2 names that now read in the amide, amino, amine, acyl and ester round trips are swept in the new file only
  (`isR6d2AmideName()`), so every earlier sweep keeps its names and exact counts (the I-52d1 precedent).
- **Chemistry:** nothing from memory; every name, form and refusal is the forward engine's.
