## What was built

- **Tiles:** «Nombres tradicionales» gains `cresol`, `pirocatecol`, `catecol`, `resorcinol`, `hidroquinona` after
  `mesitileno` (kind `template`, ids `template-cresol`, `template-pyrocatechol`, `template-catechol`,
  `template-resorcinol`, `template-hydroquinone`). TEMPLATES gives each its OH positions (`hydroxy`: `cresol` [1],
  the diols [1,2] / [1,3] / [1,4]); `cresol` has one methyl group (`positions` null, `letterOnly`), the diols none
  (`count` 0, `group` null, `positions` empty: they place everything themselves). The texts come from
  `TRADITIONAL_NAMES.oCresol` without its letter and from `BENZENE_DIGROUP_NAMES`.
- **Parser:** a retained phenol is a template parent. Its OH groups count as occupied positions for the letters, so
  `p-cresol` (OH + methyl) is two, and any prefix beside a letter on a retained phenol is three or more (POSITIONAL,
  «(contando los dos grupos OH del catecol)»; on `cresol` the review's message below). Numbers or a letter on a diol
  are TEMPLATE_LOCANTS (««catecol» ya dice dónde van sus dos grupos OH, en los carbonos 1 y 2…»); numbers on `cresol`
  are TEMPLATE_LOCANTS with their own message (letterOnlyMessage(); with prefixes, the review's message), and
  `checkLetters()` then leaves its letter alone (no second «no las dos cosas» error). A multiplier before one, or one
  inside a compound branch, is
  TILE_ORDER, the branch hint saying to write `fenil` and `hidroxi`, `(4-hidroxifenil)`; `ol` after one is out of place.
- **Interpreter / builder:** the OH groups of a retained phenol are the NameSpec's suffix, placed by the word as
  `fenol`'s is (SuffixSpec `phenol` set, one implicit slot per OH on the template tile, multiplier the OH count), so
  build.js marks their carbons as the ring's own («…: en el catecol, ese carbono ya lleva un grupo OH») and the search,
  the verdict's `suffixAtoms` and the diagnosis treat them as the engine's principal group. `cresol`'s methyl is the
  template's one group: at the letter's locant (`p-cresol`: 4; carbon 1 is the OH's) or one left-out slot.
- **Search / verdicts:** bare `cresol` is three structures (2-, 3-, 4-metilfenol; the methyl never on carbon 1), each
  filled with the letter of its position (`letterTile()`: 2/6 → `o`, 3/5 → `m`, 4 → `p`), so step 6 matches the
  engine's `o-cresol`, `m-cresol`, `p-cresol` and the `ambiguous` message, since only a letter is missing, starts «Falta
  la letra: puede ser …» (`VERDICT_TEXTS.ambiguousLetter`); a `cresol` with prefixes of the student's gets the
  review's lead instead (below).
- **Diagnosis:** the I-50d `SUBSTITUTED_TEMPLATE` rule fits a substituted retained phenol (its OH groups are the suffix
  the engine writes too, so the alcohol nulls do not fire): its message now takes the parent the engine names from —
  «benceno» for the diols (and every hydrocarbon template, text unchanged), «fenol» for `cresol`
  (`tetrametilcresol` → «… a partir de «fenol» … «2,3,4,5,6-pentametilfenol»»).
- **Messages:** `withArticle()` quotes a parent word with its article (`del tolueno`, `en el fenol`, `de la
  hidroquinona`, `en la hidroquinona`) in the POSITIONAL, LOCANT_RANGE and VALENCE messages.
- **Dialog:** tooltips (`TILE_HINTS`) for the five tiles; their text stays the accessible name.
- **Ayuda:** «Por ahora solo leo nombres de hidrocarburos, …, de sus derivados halogenados y de sus alcoholes y
  fenoles.»; the rings line lists the phenol templates and says `cresol` takes only the letter (p-cresol), with
  `4-clorocatecol` beside `4-metiltolueno`; the groups line gains the alcohol line (`ol`, `hidroxi`, `fenol`).

## Decisions

- **`pirocatecol` is a tile too:** the forward engine lists both `pirocatecol` (label «acepta») and `catecol` (label
  «nombre tradicional muy usado; la IUPAC (2013) acepta pirocatecol») as alternatives of `benceno-1,2-diol`, so both
  read as `alternative` with those labels.
- **No numeric `cresol`:** the engine writes the cresols only with their letter (`TRADITIONAL_NAMES` oCresol…pCresol;
  no `2-cresol` in any alternative, tested). As for numbers on `tolueno` or `fenol`, which the engine never writes either,
  numbers before `cresol` are refused (TEMPLATE_LOCANTS, «Con «cresol» no van números: una letra dice dónde está su
  metilo respecto al grupo OH (o-cresol, m-cresol o p-cresol).») rather than drawn as an `otherName`. Its left-out letter
  is filled as a letter and the `ambiguous` message asks for a letter, so the app never invites the student to add a
  number it then refuses.
- **Letters follow §14.4 unchanged:** a retained phenol's OH groups count as occupied positions, like a template's own
  groups (I-50c: `4-metil-o-xileno` is three). So a halogen on `p-cresol` (`2-cloro-p-cresol`, also the school name
  `4-cloro-m-cresol`) is POSITIONAL `invalid`, and so is a lettered prefix on a diol (`p-metilcatecol`). A letter on a
  diol is TEMPLATE_LOCANTS, as on `tolueno`.
- **The OH groups are the suffix, not template groups:** reading them as the NameSpec suffix placed by the word
  (`fenol`'s representation since I-51a2) keeps one model of the principal group for the builder, the search, the
  verdict's `suffixAtoms` and the diagnosis (`alcoholBeyondRules()` compares suffix counts); TemplateSpec `groups` keeps
  only carbon groups (`cresol`'s methyl; none for the diols).
- **Diagnosis:** SUBSTITUTED_TEMPLATE fits every substituted retained phenol that reaches `otherName` (a prefix beside
  `cresol`'s letter is POSITIONAL, so the substituted cresols that reach `otherName` are the letter-less ones one
  structure fills, `tetrametilcresol`); only its parent word needed a parameter (`fenol` for one OH). Deliberate, tested
  null (for R-5a3b, listed in the «Alcohols» header): `hidroxi` on a retained phenol (`4-hidroxicatecol` →
  `benceno-1,2,4-triol`), the OH-count case of I-51a2.
- **Diols carry no `id` in TEMPLATES:** their key is their `BENZENE_DIGROUP_NAMES` id; an `id: 'resorcinol'` literal
  would be a retyped tile word (`reverse-tiles.test.js` «src/reverse never retypes a tile word»).
- **Tooltips only for the new tiles:** they say nothing of their groups (unlike `tolueno`… which name a group the
  student knows); the hydrocarbon templates still have none.

## Deviations

- The brief's example «a halogen on `p-cresol`» reads as `invalid` (POSITIONAL, three positions), not `otherName`: the
  brief also asks for letters to follow §14.4 and `checkLetters()` («never with three or more positions»), as I-50c did
  for `4-metil-o-xileno`. The substituted-template `otherName` is shown with the diols and `tetrametilcresol`.
- New verdict leads beside §15.3's «Faltan números: puede ser …»: «Falta la letra: puede ser …» (a bare `cresol`) and,
  since the review, «El nombre «cresol» no admite más sustituyentes: se escribe con números. Puede ser …» (a `cresol`
  with prefixes of the student's and no letter).
- `SUBSTITUTED_TEMPLATE` data gained `parent` (its example data too); the hydrocarbon-template message is unchanged.
- The halogen e2e checked the Ayuda phrase «y de sus derivados halogenados»; the sentence now reads «…, de sus
  derivados halogenados y de sus alcoholes y fenoles», so it checks «de sus derivados halogenados».
- Not changed (pre-existing): with a left-out group, a VALENCE error found through the lowest placement counts that
  group on carbon 1 too (`1-clorocresol` «tendría 6 enlaces», as `1-clorometilfenol` already did since I-51a2); the
  three-positions message's example stays `1,2,4-trimetilbenceno` for every parent.
