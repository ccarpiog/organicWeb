## What was built

- **Tile:** «Grupos» gains `amino` after `amina`, a retained word (kind `retained`, like `ciano`), its value the
  language-neutral `aminoGroup` (the catalogue test forbids retyping a tile word in src/reverse, and the word is the same in
  Spanish and English).
- **Composition (decision):** `amino` alone is the NH₂ (`2 amino et ano 1 ol`); the groups on its nitrogen are the prefixes of a
  compound branch whose own chain is `amino`, written before it without locants, as the engine's structure has them
  (`START met il amino END` → `(metilamino)`, `START di met il amino END` → `(dimetilamino)`, `START et il met il amino END` →
  `[etil(metil)amino]`, `START START 2 cloro et il END amino END` → `[(2-cloroetil)amino]`, `bis START met il amino END` →
  `bis(metilamino)`). The marks of the prefix are the controls', the ones around every group after the first automatic. Not
  `oxi`'s closing-tile model: there `di met il oxi` is two methoxy groups, so `di met il amino` would have to mean two
  methylamino groups and `(dimetilamino)` and `[etil(metil)amino]` would have no tiles; the brief's `metil` + `amino`, `di` +
  `metil` + `amino`, `etil` + `metil` + `amino` hold inside the marks, and parentheses in the name stay START / END.
- **Grammar:** `simple = … | amino`; a compound branch's own chain may be `amino`. checkAminoGroups() refuses a locant, `N` or a
  letter on a group of the nitrogen and an acyl group on it; other chemistry is build.js's and the engine's.
- **Assembler (rule 12):** a branch whose own chain is `amino` encloses every group after the first and has no crowded rule of its
  own (render.js `groupTokens(…, enclose = g > 0)`); the prefix itself is enclosed and compound as any branch with prefixes.
- **Tokenizer:** nothing of its own (`amino` is a RET word; the automatic marks inside START / END already read).
- **Interpreter / builder / search:** SubstituentSpec kind `aminoGroup` (length 1, an implicit free valence, the groups' implicit
  slots at the one atom, like a class name's oxygen); build.js creates the nitrogen first, then its groups, checks its three bonds
  (VALENCE «El nitrógeno del grupo amino tendría 4 enlaces…», blamed on the group that adds the fourth); the search models the
  nitrogen as a one-atom chain of valence 3 and never puts an amino prefix on an amine's nitrogen.
- **Diagnosis:** an amino prefix is a hetero group (rule 1 never blames it; «sustituyentes»); its style shape is its nitrogen and
  its groups' shapes (aminoShape()); the new form rule aminoGroups() reads the groups on an amino prefix's nitrogen first
  (ALPHABETICAL_ORDER, UNGROUPED, MULTIPLIER_KIND on those lists only), because they decide the words the name-level order reads
  — found by the generated sweep: `3-[metil(metil)amino]-2,2-dimetil…` got a name-level ALPHABETICAL_ORDER ««metil» va antes que
  «metil(metil)amino»» before the UNGROUPED it needs.

## Decisions

- **Acylamino refused, `amino` + `oxo` read.** An acyl group on the nitrogen (`(acetilamino)`) is a new composition and the
  amides' (R-6d), so it is TILE_ORDER with «…un grupo C=O unido al nitrógeno no forma una amina, sino una amida» (the precedent of
  `oxi` after an acyl word before R-6a2b). An amide prefix written `amino` + `oxo` on one carbon (`ácido 4-amino-4-oxobutanoico`)
  needs no new tile and is the engine's own text, so it reads (`correct`); its rows join the round trips; a molecule whose
  principal group is an amide stays outside the diagnosis rules (outsideRules()).
- **Shared rules on the groups of the nitrogen.** The brief names "a wrong N-substituent order" among the amino prefix's own
  mistakes; ALPHABETICAL_ORDER holds there (render.js cites them in citation order, as any prefixes) and says something true
  (««etil» va antes que «metil»»), so it speaks (with UNGROUPED and MULTIPLIER_KIND) instead of a null — see Deviations.
- **The parent on the other side of an amino prefix's nitrogen** gets the chain rules' true messages (LONGER_CHAIN:
  `[(3-hidroxipropil)amino]metanol`), as an ether's oxygen did before ETHER_SIDE; a message told from the nitrogen is I-52c4's.
- **Chemistry:** nothing from memory; every name, form and refusal is the forward engine's.
