# I-39c — Ester prefixes and diesters (split from the old I-39c)

- Risk: high · worker: opus.
- Split: the old I-39c "ester and amide prefixes, pair matrix" became I-39c (ester prefixes and
  diesters, this phase) and I-39d (amide prefixes and pair matrix, queued; design §13.4).

## Decisions

- **Where the ester carbon X belongs (recorded in design §13.6; from memory, IUPAC 2013
  P-65.6.3.3).** Beside an acid (ácido > éster) an ester is a prefix, and X follows the rule of the
  acyl carbon of I-39b: it is an ordinary skeleton carbon, kept in the chain whenever the chain rules
  put it there, and then cited as `alcoxi` (its bridge O with the O-bound group) + `oxo` (its C=O) at
  X's locant: `ácido 4-metoxi-4-oxobutanoico`, never `ácido 3-(metoxicarbonil)propanoico`;
  `ácido 3-metil-4-metoxi-4-oxobutanoico` on a length tie (more prefixes, P-45.2.1); `2-metoxi-2-oxoetil`
  inside a branch. Only when the chain misses X is the whole ester `alcoxicarbonil-`
  (`ácido 3-(metoxicarbonil)pentanodioico`). No carbon joins `outsideCarbons()`, so the parent choice
  is unchanged.
- **`aciloxi-` (from memory).** An ester bonded to the chain through its bridge O is the acyl prefix of
  X + `oxi`; IUPAC 2013 prefers `acetiloxi` to `acetoxi` (not offered). X is carbon 1 of that acyl
  group, so the I-39b rules apply: `formiloxi`, `acetiloxi`, `propanoiloxi`, systematic
  `(2-cloroetanoil)oxi`. The acyl part keeps its own marks when it needs them:
  `[(2-metilpropanoil)oxi]`, `[(prop-2-enoil)oxi]` (IUPAC style, compound prefix inside a prefix).
  The pre-existing alkyl + `oxi` form without inner marks (`(4-metilhexiloxi)`, I-34) is unchanged.
- **`alcoxicarbonil-`.** The alkoxy prefix of the O-bound group + `carbonil`, following the prefix
  styles (`isopropoxicarbonil`, `[(propan-2-iloxi)carbonil]`, `[(1-metiletoxi)carbonil]`), always
  enclosed and compound (`bis(metoxicarbonil)`), alphabetised by its complete name.
- **Diesters.** Without an acid, two esters whose carbons lie on one carbon piece are both chain ends
  (P0): `-dioato`, then the O-bound groups. Identical groups once with `di` / `bis` (as prefixes are
  multiplied, IUPAC 2013 P-16.9): `butanodioato de dimetilo`, `di(propan-2-ilo)`, `di-tert-butilo`,
  `bis(2-cloroetilo)`. Different groups in alphabetical order joined by `y` (Spanish connector from
  memory; English `ethyl methyl propanedioate`): `propanodioato de etilo y metilo`.
- **What stays refused (new messages).** Without an acid: more than two esters (`manyEsters`,
  `-tricarboxilato`); two esters on different carbon pieces (`esterPrefix`, narrowed: a diol diester
  `diacetato de etano-1,2-diilo`, an ester inside the O-bound group of another — one would be a prefix,
  and which one IUPAC prefers was not decided); a diester with different O-bound groups on an acid part
  that differs seen from either end (new `mixedDiester`: `2-metilbutanodioato de 1-etilo y
  4-metilo` would need locants for the groups, whose rules were not implemented rather than guessed;
  `diesterNeedsLocants()` compares rooted tree keys of the acid part from each ester carbon). Rings
  (`ringEster`, `ringAcid`) and every amide beside an acid or ester (`amidePrefix`, I-39d) unchanged.
  An engine safety net (`esterPrefix`) checks that without an acid every ester is a suffix group.
- **No traditional name for a diester**: `carbonylTraditionalId()` now offers `formiato` / `acetato`
  for monoesters only (an ethanedioate diester was about to be called `acetato de dimetilo`).

## What was done

- Validation (`src/model/validate.js`): `esterPlacementError()`, `diesterNeedsLocants()`,
  `sideAtoms()`; the acid + ester refusal lifted; `MANY_ESTERS_MESSAGE`, `ESTER_PREFIX_MESSAGE`
  rewritten, `MIXED_DIESTER_MESSAGE` new.
- Engine: `substituent.js` (`esterAttachment()`, `esterOxoOf()`; `buildSubstituent()` flags
  `alkoxycarbonyl`, or `acyl` for X seen from its bridge O; `substituentsOf()` flags the `oxo` / `alcoxi`
  occurrences on an ester carbon `ester`, carried to the PrefixLocants), `render.js`
  (`alkoxycarbonylTokens()`, `alkoxycarbonylAlkoxy()`, acyloxy enclosure, `esterAlkylParts()`,
  `assembleEster()` over every O-bound group), `structure.js` (`esters`, `esterParts()`), `index.js`
  (every O-bound group; ester safety net), `principal.js` (traditional names), both lexicons
  (`esterAlkylJoin`, `alkoxycarbonylEnding`).
- Explanation (`src/explain/explain.js`): `esterPrefixTotal()`, `esterPrefixes()`,
  `esterPrefixSpecs()`, `chainEsterIds()`, `esterTotal()`, `esterGroups()`, `esterGroupSum()`,
  `esterAlkylSpecs()`; the acid's group step lists the esters and how each is cited
  (`esterPrefixSentences()`, `esterHow()`), never calling an ester C=O an aldehyde or its middle O an
  ether (the ether step and the acyl counts subtract them); `esterChainSentences()`;

  carbon inside a branch; legend, order and assemble sentences; diesters: group step, a new step

  once), `diesterGroupsSentence()`, `diesterGroupLegend()`; each ester highlighted whole.
- Oracle: `esterGraft()`, `generateEsterPrefixes()` (`scripts/oracle/generate.mjs`), wired into
  `run.mjs`.
- Tests: `tests/unit/ester-prefixes.test.js` (10 tests), `tests/e2e/ester-prefixes.spec.js`
  (3 × source/dist), 35 fixture rows (`# Ester prefixes and diesters (I-39c)`), 4 new explanation
  snapshots, the group snapshots of the old refusals replaced by still-refused molecules
  (`CC(=O)OCCOC(C)=O`, two ring acids); old `esterPrefix` / `manyEsters` assertions updated in
  `esters`, `cyano`, `ethers`, `groups` unit tests and `esters.spec.js`, `acids.spec.js`; oracle test
  counts (56 skipped, 1531 passed).
- Docs: design §3.2, §4.1 error table, §5, §8, §13.4 (I-35 notes, I-39c **Done.**, new I-39d row),
  §13.6; README; help text in `index.html`.
