- Risk: high · worker: opus · reviewer: Codex (`docs/reviews/I-39a.md`, ship-with-fixes).
- Split: I-39 (v2 §3.19) became I-39a `ciano-` prefix, I-39b acyl prefixes
  (`formil-`/`acetil-`), I-39c ester/amide prefixes + pair matrix (design §13.4).

## What was done

- `outsideCarbons()` (`src/naming/principal.js`) lists carbons that are never chain carbons
  (today the `ciano-` nitrile carbons; I-39b adds the acyl carbons). Parent selection works on
  the skeleton without them (`parent.js`); any nitrile carbon hanging from a chain is the
  simple prefix `ciano` (C + N) at any depth (`substituent.js`, `render.js`).
- Named now: `ácido 3-cianopropanoico`, `ácido 3-ciano-2-metilpentanoico`,
  `etanoato de cianometilo`, `3-(cianometoxi)propanonitrilo`, `ácido 2,3,4-tricianobutanoico`,
  `ácido 2-cianoetanoico` (existing locant rules keep the 2).
- Refusals: `cyanoPrefix` removed (the engine safety net now checks that every nitrile is a
  suffix or a `ciano`); `manyNitriles` narrowed to 3+ nitriles as the principal group on one
  piece (`-tricarbonitrilo`); new `carbonocyanidic` for NC–C(=O)X (`ácido carbonocianídico`,
  P-65.2.1, from memory). `N#CCOCC#N` / `N#CCNCC#N` refused as `symmetricEther` /
  `symmetricAmine`. `esterPrefix`, `manyEsters`, `amidePrefix`, `acylSubstituent`,
  `ringNitrile` unchanged.
- Explanation: formula counts, group step (seniority with `nitrilo`), chain step (the –C≡N
  carbon is not part of the chain), substituents and legend; the whole –C≡N highlighted.
- Oracle: `generateCyano()` (`scripts/oracle/generate.mjs`).
- Docs: design §13.4 rows I-39a/b/c, §13.6, README, help text in `index.html`.

## Decisions from memory

- `ciano-` includes the nitrile carbon (IUPAC 2013 P-66.5).
- NC–COOH / NC–C(=O)X are carbonic-acid derivatives, refused (`carbonocyanidic`).
