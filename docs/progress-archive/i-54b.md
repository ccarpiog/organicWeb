## Decisions

- **Identical prefixes with one letter (`p-metilmetilbenceno`) are read**, not refused: "different" is chemistry, not
  syntax (`metil` and `(metan-1-il)` are one branch). The molecule is drawn and UNGROUPED says «p-dimetil», no
  «p-metilmetil».
- **`clorometilbenceno` keeps its message** «Faltan números: puede ser 1-cloro-2-metilbenceno, …». The `ambiguous` message
  lists the molecules the name can describe, not ways of writing it; `dimetilbenceno` never mentioned the letter either.
- **The `different` message is reworded, not removed**, for the one case it still refused (a letter on `benceno` with a group
  after it); it now points at the whole word that takes the letter.
- **pairMixed marks the letter** (with its own prefix's numbers when they share a prefix), not the other prefix's numbers:
  the error range must be contiguous, and the message says «no las dos cosas».
- **RING_NUMBERING gives way to ALPHABETICAL_ORDER** for the letter form; the alphabetical message gains the right form only
  for this case (the general wording, D-9, is unchanged).
- **Closed-loop floors raised** for the three generators that meet the letter forms, so a lost form shows up as a coverage drop.
- HINTS.positional (E-18 / E-48: «…van delante de los dos grupos del benceno que sitúan…») left as it is: still true.
