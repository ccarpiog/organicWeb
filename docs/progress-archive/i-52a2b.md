## What was built

- **No new tile.** An acyloxy prefix is an acyl branch closed by `oxi` (`acetil oxi`, `formil oxi`, `benzoil oxi`, `prop ano oil
  oxi`, `ciclo hex ano carbonil oxi`); an alkoxycarbonyl prefix an alkoxy branch closed by `oxi` and then `carbonil` (`met il oxi
  carbonil`, `isopropil oxi carbonil`, `prop ano 2 il oxi carbonil`, `fenil oxi carbonil`); with prefixes of its own the inner
  branch is a compound branch, and both endings go before its END, exactly as `oxi` does for an alkoxy prefix (`START 2 met il
  prop ano oil oxi END` → `[(2-metilpropanoil)oxi]`, `START 2 cloro et il oxi carbonil END` → `[(2-cloroetoxi)carbonil]`).
- **Parser:** `oxi` after an acyl head is accepted (tiles.js isAcyloxyHead()); `carbonil` right after `oxi` is the node's
  `carbonil` field, set only when the tile is there (every other tree keeps its shape and the deepEqual tests stand).
- **Assembler (rule 11):** both prefixes always enclosed and compound (`(acetiloxi)`, `bis(metoxicarbonil)`); the inner part — the
  acyl prefix before `oxi`, the alkoxy prefix before `carbonil` — gets automatic marks one level inside when render.js encloses it
  (`[(prop-2-enoil)oxi]`, `[(ciclohexanocarbonil)oxi]`, `[(propan-2-iloxi)carbonil]`; always with prefixes of its own), the inner
  opening mark right after the outer one (an automatic one, or the START control's), the inner closing mark right before `oxi` or
  `carbonil`: text order stays tile order, one span per tile, the marks automatic text.
- **Tokenizer:** `oxi` after OIL, CARB and RET; `carbonil` after `oxi` (ALKCARB); an automatic enclosure may start with RET and hold
  it with its `oxi`, and take `carbonil` after `oxi`; an `inner` mark opens right inside an automatic opening mark, or (`compound`)
  right after a START control, read like that branch's content, and closes only after an acyl part (then `oxi`) or an alkoxy
  part's `oxi` (then `carbonil`). Every reading is still checked by parse + assemble (readsAs()).
- **Interpreter / builder / search:** an acyloxy prefix is its acyl branch's SubstituentSpec with `alkoxy` too (the C=O built
  first, then the ether oxygen bonded to the C=O carbon, the oxygen the occurrence's attachment); an alkoxycarbonyl prefix the
  alkoxy group's with `alkoxycarbonyl` (its ether oxygen, then a new C=O carbon with its =O, the attachment; one more carbon and
  two more heavy atoms per occurrence). The search needs no new role: each takes one bond of the carbon it hangs from.
- **Diagnosis:** the rules read the prefixes as prefixes. specShape() gives them the engine's shapes, so the prefix style is
  recognised (`[(propan-2-iloxi)carbonil]` → the pin style). Checked message by message: ALPHABETICAL_ORDER («acetiloxi» va antes
  que «metil»), UNGROUPED («2,2-bis(acetiloxi)»), ALKOXY_MULTIPLIER for `di(acetiloxi)`, MULTIPLIER_KIND for
  `di(metoxicarbonil)`, NEEDLESS_LOCANTS inside (`[(propan-1-iloxi)carbonil]`), ACYL_CHAIN (`[(3-metilpropanoil)oxi]`),
  BRANCH_RING_NUMBERING (`[(6-metilbenzoil)oxi]`, `[(ciclohexano-2-carbonil)oxi]`, `[(6-metilfenoxi)carbonil]`), NUMBERING,
  BRANCH_AT_END, MORE_SUBSTITUENTS, LONGER_CHAIN, MORE_MULTIPLE_BONDS, RING_NUMBERING.
- **Dialog / Ayuda:** the `oxi` tooltip ends «Detrás de una rama con un C=O forma un éster como rama: acetil y oxi dan
  (acetiloxi).», the `carbonil` one «Detrás de «oxi» forma un éster como rama: met, il, oxi y carbonil dan (metoxicarbonil).»; the
  Ayuda ester paragraph ends with the prefix sentence.

## Decisions

- **Composition: reuse, «inside».** `oxi` after the acyl words and endings of R-5c2, `carbonil` after `oxi`, both before a compound
  branch's END like `oxi` in I-51b1 (`(2-metilpropoxi)`), so one rule covers alkoxy and ester prefixes («oxi va dentro de la rama
  compuesta, antes de cerrarla») and the Ayuda keeps one wording. Rejected: new whole-word tiles (`acetiloxi`, `metoxicarbonil`:
  they would not compose with substituted or long groups), a new `alcoxicarbonil` ending tile (the lexicon's `carbonil` is the
  same word, ALKOXYCARBONYL_ENDING = RING_CARBONYL_ENDING), and `oxi` / `carbonil` after the END of a compound branch («outside»:
  the student's marks would be the inner ones, but a second field on the branch node and a mark that encloses a compound branch
  in the tokenizer; text order is kept either way). The inner marks are the app's, as every enclosing mark of a simple branch.
- **`carbonil` after an acyloxy prefix is refused** (the oxygen between two C=O groups, an anhydride the engine refuses), with its
  own hint; `carbonil` after a compound branch's END gets a hint that sends it inside. No new parse code: TILE_ORDER fits.
- **Multipliers:** an alkoxycarbonyl prefix is a C=O carbon with groups of its own (render.js isCompoundPrefix() says compound
  always), so `di(metoxicarbonil)` gets MULTIPLIER_KIND («Delante de una rama que tiene sus propias ramas…»); an acyloxy prefix is
  an `oxi` prefix that is not shortened, ALKOXY_MULTIPLIER's non-tert-butyl message. **Fixed:** ALKOXY_MULTIPLIER chose the
  `tert-butoxi` wording for any retained word before `oxi` (until this phase only `tert-butil` could reach it), so `di(acetiloxi)`
  said «su forma sistemática, «1,1-dimetiletoxi»»; it now asks for `tert-butil` itself.
- **The engine's words with their split mark:** the forward engine's `parts` keep an ester prefix's inner opening mark as a
  separate punctuation part (`[`, `(`, `2-metilbenzoil)oxi`), so the branch-name rules quoted an unbalanced «2-metilbenzoil)oxi».
  partText() puts back the opening marks the part's text leaves unmatched (no change for any other prefix); the forward engine is
  not touched.
- **Ester prefix words a deliberate null:** esterPrefixWords(), first of the branch-name checks, answers BEYOND when the student's
  branch and the engine's on the same atoms are not the same kind of ester prefix (`(1-oxoetoxi)` for `(acetiloxi)`,
  `[(metoxi)oxometil]` for `(metoxicarbonil)`), or an acyloxy group's acyl part has the general rule's word (`(etanoiloxi)`,
  `(metanoiloxi)`: ACYL_NAME's message speaks of a branch bonded by its C=O carbon, which is not what the student wrote). **Fixed
  by it:** `ácido 2-(1-oxometoxi)etanoico` (readable since R-5b1) got NEEDLESS_LOCANTS «se escribe «2-(oxometoxi)»», a name the
  engine never writes; now a `words` null.
- **On benzene:** beside an acid the engine refuses an ester prefix on a benzene ring (`ácido 4-(acetiloxi)benzoico`, `ácido
  2-[4-(metoxicarbonil)fenil]etanoico` → `outOfScope` with its message); a benzene inside the prefix reads (`(benzoiloxi)`,
  `(fenoxicarbonil)`, `[(4-metilbenzoil)oxi]`); without an acid the molecule is the ester itself (`(acetiloxi)benceno` →
  «etanoato de fenilo», a `seniority` null).
- **`bis-(tert-butoxicarbonil)` as the engine writes it:** render.js groupTokens() puts rule 5's hyphen after a multiplier whenever
  the prefix's words start with an italic descriptor, also inside its enclosing mark (`ácido
  2,3-bis-(tert-butoxicarbonil)pentanodioico`), so the assembler does too (an opening mark right before an italic word takes its
  flag; the tokenizer reads the hyphen there, OPEN_ITALIC). The same hyphen was missing before a crowded alkoxy prefix's mark
  (R-5b): the engine's `ácido 4-[bis-(tert-butoxi)fluorometil]hexanoico` did not tokenize, and now reads `correct`. See the open
  question below.
- **Sweeps apart:** the acid and acyl sweeps keep their names and exact counts (isAcidName() still means «no ester prefix»); the
  ester-prefix names are swept in the new test file.
- **Chemistry:** nothing from memory; every name, form and refusal is the forward engine's.

## Deliberate nulls («Esters», for I-52a3)

- `inChain`: an alkoxycarbonyl prefix whose C=O carbon the engine puts in the parent chain, the student's chain lengthened through
  it (the engine writes the ester as `alcoxi` and `oxo`: `ácido 3-(metoxicarbonil)propanoico` → «ácido 4-metoxi-4-oxobutanoico»,
  `ácido 6-(metoxicarbonil)hexanoico` → «ácido 7-metoxi-7-oxoheptanoico»). When the engine's chain merely passes through that
  carbon and leaves a student's carbon out, the chain rules speak (MORE_SUBSTITUENTS, LONGER_CHAIN) and it is no null.
- `words`: an ester prefix written as another kind of branch, or the reverse (`(1-oxoetoxi)`, `(1-oxometoxi)`,
  `[(metoxi)oxometil]`), and an acyloxy group's acyl with the general word (`(etanoiloxi)`, `(metanoiloxi)`) (esterPrefixWords()).
- `seniority`: an ester prefix beside no group senior to an ester — the molecule is the ester itself (`(acetiloxi)metano` →
  «etanoato de metilo», `2-(acetiloxi)heptanal`, `(benzoiloxi)benceno` → «benzoato de fenilo», `(acetiloxi)benceno`,
  `(metoxicarbonil)benceno`, `3-(metoxicarbonil)pentan-2-ona`) (esterChecks(), as I-52a1's `seniority`).
- **Counts:** NULLS test 13 cases (2 `inChain`, 5 `words`, 6 `seniority`). Fixture sweep (24 names): 31 `otherName` (shifted 2,
  reflected 1, rotated 20, swapped 2, unsorted 1, ungrouped 2, diBis 2, heteroToEnd 1), 28 diagnosed, 3 `inChain` nulls.
  Generated sweep (282 names): 853 `otherName`, 789 diagnosed, 64 `inChain` nulls. No unexpected null. The I-52a1 counts (fixture
  94 of 155, generated 407 of 1040, carbonyl fixture 8), I-52a2a's (9 / 54) and every other family's are unchanged.

## Deviations / open risks

- **Not an «Esters» null, a pre-existing pattern:** `ácido 3-[(1,1-dimetiletoxi)carbonil]pentanodioico` is `otherName` with no
  diagnosis, like `1-(1,1-dimetiletoxi)butano`: the forward engine lists the prefix-style alternatives only beside an isopropyl
  group (index.js STYLE_DEPENDENT_PREFIXES), so the substituted style of a tert-butyl group is never offered. Tested as it is.
- **Open question (forward engine, not changed):** render.js writes `bis-(tert-butoxicarbonil)` and `bis-(tert-butoxi)` (the hyphen
  before the enclosing mark); IUPAC usage is `bis(tert-butoxycarbonyl)` (from memory; the hyphen goes between a multiplier and an
  italic descriptor written right after it, `di-tert-butyl`). The reverse engine follows the engine, as I-51b1 followed its
  `bis-tert-butoxi`; a fix belongs to `src/naming/render.js` groupTokens() (user's call).
- ACYL_NAME is not extended to an acyloxy group's acyl word: `(etanoiloxi)` stays a `words` null for I-52a3.
- An ester prefix on a benzene ring beside an acid, which the brief lists («on `benceno`»), is the engine's refusal (`outOfScope`).
- A stray `oil`, `carbonil` or `ilo` tile had no part kind (`part-undefined` on the name line, since R-5c2 / R-6a1); now 'ending'.
- design.md §15.8's Status column is not updated (the user's uncommitted file).
