## What was built

- **The branch-name rules** (`branchNames()`, first of FORM_RULES: after the parent's rules 1–4, before
  ALPHABETICAL_ORDER, rule number 5). On the same parent as the engine's (`sameAtoms()`), a parent-level prefix whose words
  (`wordsOf()`) are not the engine's `prefix` part for the same atoms is searched, from itself inward (`prefixNodes()`, the
  first occurrence of each node, `ctx.occurrences`), for a branch whose own name the engine writes otherwise; the first
  check that names it gives the draft: focus the branch (`occurrenceIds()` at the parent level, `branchIds()` deeper),
  tiles the branch's prefix node; the message quotes the engine's and the student's words of the parent-level prefix,
  without its locants.
  - **ACYL_NAME** (`acylName()`): a branch bonded by the carbon of a C=O of its own, which the engine names as an acyl group
    (`engineBranch()`: substituent.js `nameSubstituent()` in the diagnosis's style), written `etanoil` / `metanoil` (the
    named acyl groups, `acetil` / `formil`) or as an `oxo` alkyl group (`(1-oxoetil)`, `(1-oxopropil)`,
    `(ciclohexiloxometil)` → `-carbonil`).
  - **ACYL_CHAIN** (`acylChain()`): an `oil` acyl group whose chain (the first atoms build() made for it) is not the
    engine's acyl chain; `longer` when the engine's has more carbons.
  - **BRANCH_RING_NUMBERING** (`branchRingNumbering()`): a ring in a branch (`fenil`, `ciclohexil`, `fenoxi`, `benzoil`,
    `…carbonil`; `engineRing()`) whose numbering is not the engine's: its carbon 1 not the bonded one (`start`), or its own
    prefixes (grouped by graph.js `rootedBranchKey()`) or a `ciclo` ring's multiple bonds (`ringBondLocant()`) located
    otherwise. Reason `bonds` / `bondsOnly` / `lower` / `alphabetical` (same locant set, other assignment), none for a bare
    ring, whose 1 is not even written (`unwritten`). (The aromaticity, the reason and the alphabetical safeguard were
    corrected after the review: «Review fix».)
- **Rule 9 variants:** `traditionalLocants()` — a template name whose parent-level prefix locants left out give one of the
  engine's names (`2-fenilacetaldehído` → `fenilacetaldehído`), NEEDLESS_LOCANTS `traditional`, step `assemble` (it lists
  the traditional name); `carbonylLocant()` — `carbonil` without the locant 1 on a ring with a multiple bond or own prefixes
  (render.js `ringCarbonylTokens()`; the reverse of `shortForm()`), MISSING_LOCANTS `branch`, the right form the student's
  prefix with a number tile 1 added, step `substituents`.

## Decisions

- **Three new codes, two reused ones.** No rule judged a compound branch's own chain or numbering (hydrocarbon branches are
  «No rule covers»), and the parent's codes (BRANCH_AT_END, LONGER_CHAIN, NUMBERING, RING_NUMBERING) speak of «la cadena
  principal» and carry rule numbers 1–4, which would rank a branch mistake below a parent mistake in the kindest-reading
  comparison although it is checked after the parent's rules. A needless or missing locant is exactly
  NEEDLESS_LOCANTS / MISSING_LOCANTS, so those got variants.
- **Before ALPHABETICAL_ORDER, rule 5.** A branch's name decides the words the order reads (`2-(1-oxoetil)-2-etiloct-7-enal`
  was ALPHABETICAL_ORDER in the student's own words; the fix is `acetil`, after which the order is right). The explanation
  also names the substituents (step 5) before ordering them (step 6). They are no R-3 rules.
- **The engine's words from its naming's `parts`**, never retyped and without render.js (the purity test forbids it), and
  only on the same parent (otherwise its branches are other atoms).
- **ACYL_NAME before ACYL_CHAIN before BRANCH_RING_NUMBERING**, outer node first: an acyl group the student did not
  recognise is the main mistake; a nested acyl word inside a ring branch numbered right (`2-(2-metanoilciclohexil)…`) quotes
  the whole parent-level prefix («2-formilciclohexil»).
- **Ring numbering compared on the molecule**, not on the text: a symmetric numbering giving the same locants is no mistake
  (`(2,6-dimetilbenzoil)`); the alphabetical tie-break is blamed only with the same letters on both sides (as
  `judgedInOwnWords()` guards the parent's N4); a lower-locant mistake beside a branch in a style the engine does not offer
  is still named, and the quoted engine words then show its style (`5-(1,1-dimetiletil)fenil` → «3-tert-butilfenil»).
- **Generic, not carbonyl-only.** A ring branch hangs from a chain parent only beside a principal group (a second ring is
  refused; a hydrocarbon's ring is its parent), so an alcohol's ring branch gets BRANCH_RING_NUMBERING too
  (`2-(5-metilfenil)etan-1-ol`, formerly null; recorded in a test).
- **ESO wording** close to the explanation's own sentences (explain.js `acylIntro()`, `ringDescription()`,
  `ringCarbonylDescription()`, `benzeneOwnPrefixSentences()`), each step opened saying the rule (tested).

## Review fix

Codex review (`docs/reviews/I-51c3b2.md`): two should-fix findings, no blockers, both in BRANCH_RING_NUMBERING.

- **Should-fix 1 (`branchRingNumbering()`): a benzene's aromaticity was read from the student's spelling.**
  `2-(6-metilciclohexa-1,3,5-trien-1-il)heptanal` was told to give the double bonds the lowest locants first (its Kekulé
  bonds counted, the student having written `ciclo`). Fix: the ring is a benzene when the engine recognises it (its ring
  structure's `retained` 'benzene', naming/aromatic.js), whatever the spelling; its Kekulé bonds never count, and a benzene
  written with `ciclo` gets the sentence «Ese anillo es un benceno: sus enlaces dobles no cuentan para numerar.» (`benzene`).
  Both alternating patterns (`…-1,3,5-trien-1-il`, `…-2,4,6-trien-1-il`), `(6-metilfenil)` and
  `(6-metilciclohexa-1,3,5-trieno-1-carbonil)` are BRANCH_RING_NUMBERING «…a sus sustituyentes: «2-metilfenil»…»; their
  mirrors, numbered as the engine numbers the benzene (`(2-metilciclohexa-1,3,5-trien-1-il)`,
  `(ciclohexa-1,3,5-trieno-1-carbonil)`), stay null: no rule names a benzene written with `ciclo` in a branch (the
  parent's BENZENE_RING has no branch form; header «Aldehydes and ketones», «Still null»).
- **Should-fix 2 (`branchRingNumbering()`): the alphabetical safeguard was skipped when the multiple bonds tie.**
  `2-[2-cloro-6-(1,1-dimetiletil)ciclohexa-2,5-dien-1-il]propanal` (bonds 2,5 and substituents 2,6 both ways) was
  BRANCH_RING_NUMBERING with the bond reason. Fix: the reason is the first criterion that differs — the multiple bonds'
  locants, then the prefixes' locant set, then the alphabetical tie-break — and the tie-break is blamed only when the
  engine's numbering also wins in the student's own words (new `losesInOwnWords()`: the ring's own prefixes grouped by
  structure, cited in the order of the student's words, each numbering's locants per group compared, as
  `judgedInOwnWords()` does for the parent's N4). This replaces the "same letters" gate, which also blocked a right
  diagnosis when the student wrote the prefixes out of order: `2-(2-cloro-6-tert-butilciclohexa-2,5-dien-1-il)propanal`
  and `2-(6-tert-butil-2-clorociclohexa-2,5-dien-1-il)propanal` are BRANCH_RING_NUMBERING (alphabetical); the reproduction
  and its mirror `2-[6-cloro-2-(1,1-dimetiletil)…]` are null; the engine's `2-(2-tert-butil-6-clorociclohexa-2,5-dien-1-il)
  propanal` is `correct`.
- **Same flaw elsewhere (checked: ACYL_NAME, ACYL_CHAIN, the ring-carbonyl paths):**
