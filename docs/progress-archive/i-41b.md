## Acceptance (met)

- Off-by-default toggle "Abreviar CHO y COOH" (button, `aria-pressed`), shown only while the
  90° view is on, remembered in localStorage `organicWeb.abbreviations` (`ABBREVIATION_KEY` in
  `src/ui/autosave.js`; UI in `src/ui/canvasbar.js`, wiring in `src/ui/app.js`).
- Qualifying –CHO / –COOH groups drawn as one label (`OHC`/`HOOC` when the bond comes from the
  right, always at locant 1), oxygen part red; Spanish `aria-label` and `<title>` per label.
- Mapping: highlights and blue/red rings on any group atom box the whole label; clicks on the
  label hit the group's carbon; group O atoms and inner bonds are never hittable.
- Display only: model, undo history, autosave, canonical key and name unchanged; toggling off
  restores the per-atom drawing exactly (unit + e2e tests).
- Collision-free: column step from the widest real label; `rightAngleProblems()` takes the
  groups as an optional third argument; no clean placement → per-atom drawing.

## Decisions

- Qualifying group: carbon with =O plus one H or one –OH and exactly one other neighbour, a
  carbon on a single bond (always a chain or branch end). Methanal, formic acid, esters,
  formates, amides, acyl halides and ketones stay per atom; ethanedial/oxalic acid get a label
  on each carbon; rings keep falling back from the 90° view.
- Every edit tool on the label acts on the group's carbon (Enlace simple on CHO grows a carbon
  → ketone; a full COOH carbon is refused with the valence message); Borrar removes the whole
  group in one undo step.
- Labels centred on the carbon; on a vertical branch the bond meets the middle of "COOH"
  (keeps the collision grid uniform).
- No box/lasso selection exists in the 90° view (Mover is off there), so "selection" = click.
- The SVG canvas is `role="img"`, so screen readers do not reach individual labels; the
  per-label `aria-label`/`<title>` serve tooltips and tests.
