## What was built

- **The amine's own checks** (`amineChecks()`, still first in `firstRule()`):
  - **CARBONYL_SENIORITY with the junior `amine`** — a student's `amina`, `anilina` or `bencilamina` while the engine's
    principal group is another. amineChecks() names it beside an alcohol or an amide (they have no checks of their own before
    the rules); beside an acid, an ester, a nitrile, an aldehyde or a ketone their own checks reach it first (so ACID_GROUP,
    ESTER_GROUP keep their place). Where the amine goes (`amineJunior()`): an amino prefix on the parent — `amino` for a nitrogen
    bonded to one carbon, else the engine's own words (`aminoWordOf()`: `dimetilamino`, `etil(metil)amino`) —, in an ester's
    O-bound group (`side` 'oBound'), or no group apart when its nitrogen is the amide's (`inAmide`: «el grupo amino no va aparte:
    su nitrógeno, con el C=O unido a él, forma la amida»).
  - **CARBONYL_PREFIX** — a principal amine group written as an amino prefix (`ownGroupAtom()`: the prefix's nitrogen is one of
    the engine's suffix nitrogens); with groups on that nitrogen the message adds that they go before the name with `N`.
- **AMINE_SIDE** (new, `acrossNitrogen()`, ETHER_SIDE's twin): the parent-choice draft (LONGER_CHAIN, MORE_SUBSTITUENTS,
  CARBONYL_PARENT…) re-read from the principal amine's nitrogen when the student's chain is another group on it; the reason in
  etherSide()'s words (CARBONYL_PARENT with no `RINGCHAIN` step read as RING_IS_PARENT: «en ese lado hay un anillo, y un anillo
  manda siempre sobre una cadena abierta»), the holder «Tu cadena es el sustituyente «metil», que va unido al nitrógeno y se
  escribe con la letra «N» delante», the step that says why each side lost.
- **The rules with the amino group's words** (`group` 'amine' through NAMED_GROUPS): CARBONYL_PARENT, MORE_CARBONYL_GROUPS (P0),
  ETHER_SIDE told from either, NUMBERING / RING_NUMBERING / CHAIN_LOCANTS at N0, NEEDLESS_LOCANTS (`suffixWhat()`; beside an `N`
  only the suffix's locants, `nitrogen`), MISSING_LOCANTS.
- **`N` is no number**: LOCANT_ORDER's `nitrogen` clause; parse.js LOCANT_COUNT counts letters N and numbers apart.

## Decisions

- **Wording: «grupo amino» / «grupos amino»**, the explanation's SUFFIX_GROUP_WORDS.amine (not «–NH₂», which fits only a primary
  amine); the amide «la amida (un C=O unido a un nitrógeno)», generic for –CONH₂, –CONH–, –CON–.
- **Codes: the shared ones, one new code.** As the nitrile phase: CARBONYL_SENIORITY, CARBONYL_PREFIX, CARBONYL_PARENT,
  MORE_CARBONYL_GROUPS, NUMBERING / RING_NUMBERING at N0, NEEDLESS_LOCANTS / MISSING_LOCANTS take `group` 'amine'; AMINE_SIDE is
  the one new code, because no shared code says the chain cannot cross the nitrogen.
- **AMINE_SIDE as a wrapper, not a check:** the side of the nitrogen is told from the trace's own drop (as ETHER_SIDE), so a side
  that reaches the presentation tie-break (`N-etiletan-1-amina`, both sides ethyl) is no mistake — the old `side` null fired
  there whenever the engine's tie-break chose the other ethyl. The rule number is the replaced draft's (the kindest-reading
  ranking unchanged).
- **Another nitrogen is the parent choice, not the side:** `1-(4-aminociclohexil)metanamina` and
  `2-(4-aminociclohexil)etan-1-amina` are CARBONYL_PARENT («…llevan uno cada uno, así que gana el anillo»); the tests' category
  functions now read `side` only for the same nitrogen, `parent` for another (formerly both `side`).
- **`bencilamina` with a group on its nitrogen is SUBSTITUTED_TEMPLATE**, as `N-metilbencilamina` already was: the word admits no
  prefix, the plainest message; side-of-nitrogen reasoning would need the template's implicit chain explained.
- **A junior amine beside an amide is CARBONYL_SENIORITY**, its nitrogen the amide's (`N-acetiletanamina`, `1-oxobutan-1-amina`,
  `N-benzoiletanamida` with «benzamida»); the amide group step it opens says «El C=O y el nitrógeno forman un solo grupo». An
  amide with no student `amina` (`1-aminobutanal`) stays outside the rules (R-6d).
- **Seniority: the whole order** (`amineSeniority`, equal to naming/seniority.js SENIORITY in Spanish words) in every message with
  a junior amine, as the brief and the nitrile / amide group steps say it.
- **The amine nulls kept their rule rank; now real codes do**, so diagnose()'s latest-rule-among-readings choice is unchanged
  (`N-metil-N-metiletanamina` UNGROUPED beside a reading at N0).
- **Chemistry:** nothing from memory; every name, form and order is the forward engine's.

## Deviations / open questions

- `N-etilbencilamina`, `N-propilbencilamina` (listed under `side`) are SUBSTITUTED_TEMPLATE, not AMINE_SIDE (Decisions).
- The whole seniority order is shown beside an alcohol, a ketone, an aldehyde, an acid or an ester, whose group steps list a
  shorter order with the amine («alcohol > amina», «aldehído > cetona > alcohol > amina»…); a teacher may prefer the step's.
- A parent on the other side of a junior amine's nitrogen (an amino prefix's: `[(3-hidroxipropil)amino]metanol`) keeps the chain
  rules' own true messages (LONGER_CHAIN); AMINE_SIDE reads the principal amine's nitrogen only.
- On benzene AMINE_SIDE opens «Reconoce el benceno», which says the groups on the nitrogen go before with `N` but not that a ring
  beats a chain (the message says it; on `ciclo` the ring step says both).
- An amino prefix's words are quoted without their outer marks («etil(metil)amino», «dimetilamino»), as every prefix quote.
- The parser's POSITIONAL message on `anilina` («…escribe los números (N,4-dimetilanilina)») still puts the N among «los números»;
  outside the two codes the brief named.
- design.md §15.8's Status column is not updated (the user's uncommitted file).
- No forward-engine bug found; nothing in `src/naming/` or `src/explain/` changed.
- **Review fix (Codex should-fix, end branch across the nitrogen):** `N-propil-1-metilmetanamina` was BRANCH_AT_END «…la cadena
  principal tiene 3 carbonos», though its methanamine lengthened by the `metil` has 2 and the propyl parent is across the
