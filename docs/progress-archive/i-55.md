# I-55 — «¿Cómo se dibuja?»: a clear message when a multiplier tile stands where a root goes

Spec: `autoclaude/processed/250-multiplier-as-root-message.md` (user report, 2026-11-09). The tiles `4 met il ciclo hexa 1
eno` got RING_ROOT on the `ciclo` tile (plus NO_PARENT and TILE_ORDER cascades) and `octa ano` the generic «di», «tri»… hint;
neither said that `hexa` is a multiplier and that the root is `hex`. Commit fd63b26 had only reordered the rows and added
their hints. This phase changes the reader only; the forward engine and every main name are unchanged.

## Decisions

- **A new code, MULT_AS_ROOT**, rather than new wording under RING_ROOT / TILE_ORDER: easier to assert, and it marks a
  different tile than RING_ROOT did.
- **`tri` and `tetra` after `ciclo` get the message too** («La raíz de tres carbonos es «prop», de la fila «Raíces»:
  ciclopropano.»): after `ciclo` a root is required, both are ring sizes, the reading is clean (no RING_SIZE), and the
  message is more useful than RING_ROOT's list; they get no sentence on the `a` (`tri` is no `prop` + `a`). `di` keeps
  RING_ROOT (a ring of two carbons is impossible); so do `bis`, `tris`….
- **Where a chain starts, only look-alikes**, as the item says: `tri ano`, `tetra ano`, `1 3 di eno` keep today's errors.
- **Place (3), a branch's root**: the same rule in every parseItem() context. Extension beyond the item's draft: after
  numbers or a letter the multiplier also counts as a root when a branch's ending follows (`3 hexa il non ano`,
  `2 hexa 2 il`, `6 penta 1 eno 3 il`), since a multiplier is never followed by `il` / `ilo` / `oil`; before an ending
  with nothing of a branch after it, it does not (`2 hexa eno`, `1 3 5 7 9 penta eno` keep today's errors: the numbers may
  belong to a root left out, as in `1 3 di eno`).
- **After a multiplier of its own** (review fix) any continuation of a root counts, since two multipliers in a row never
  read: the multiplier stays the prefix's (`di hexa il éter` reads `dihexil éter`, MULT_AS_ROOT alone). Before a parent's
  ending the multiplier is a mistake of its own, recorded as it is before the right root (`di hexa ano`: the
  legacy-multiplier TILE_ORDER on `di`, then MULT_AS_ROOT), so there MULT_AS_ROOT is the second error, by position.
- **The extra sentence on the `a`** only when the chain has an unsaturation ending (`eno` / `ino`) — where the student is
  likely to have seen `ciclohexa-1,3-dieno` — and only for look-alikes; `octa ano` ends at «…: octano.».
- **The example is the student's own chain** (not the whole name) with the root, or `ciclohexano` / `octano` when that chain
  is unfinished or has another error; the next error in tile order (e.g. LOCANT_COUNT) is still recorded and marked.
- **ROOT_END is not added** for a multiplier root: MULT_AS_ROOT's example shows the ending, and ROOT_END (which starts at
  `ciclo`) would otherwise come first by position.
- **Order of the errors**: still by position, except that a MULT_AS_ROOT moves before an error of a larger unit that holds
  its tile (`multAsRootFirst()`: `2 hexa il` alone, whose ENDS_WITH_BRANCH starts at the `2`; `2 START hexa il`, BRANCH_OPEN).
  An error wholly before it keeps its place (`2 3 hexa il non ano`: LOCANT_COUNT on `2 3` first). Both reproductions have
  MULT_AS_ROOT as their only error.
- **Name line**: the assembler is unchanged, so the line keeps the student's tiles; with a multiplied first ending the `a`
  the app adds shows doubled (`ciclohexaa-1,3-dieno`, before I-55 `ciclohexa-1,3-dieno`), which the message's last sentence
  explains. Other lines are unchanged except where the recovered chain gets its automatic punctuation
  (`(hexaan-2-il)butano`).
- Row names are written in parse.js (`ROOT_ROW`, `MULTIPLIER_ROW`), as «Anillos» and «Nombres tradicionales» already are; a
  unit test checks them against `ROWS` (`src/ui/name-builder.js`).
