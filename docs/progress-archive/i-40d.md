## What was built

- `ringEster` retired. A ring on the acid side names the ring `-carboxilato` (I-40b machinery);
  a ring on the O side is the O-bound ring group (`fenilo`, `2-metilciclohexilo`).
- `benzoato de fenilo`: the only two-ring molecules admitted — exactly two separate simple
  carbocycles, one ester between them, no acid (`esterRingSplit()` in `validate.js`). The engine
  names both parts as one-ring molecules and joins them (`naming/index.js`). Needed a two-ring
  canonical key (`graph.js`), oracle comparison (`scripts/oracle/compare.mjs`) and Ordenar dibujo
  layout (`src/layout/canonical.js`).
- Oracle generator `generateRingEsters()` (`scripts/oracle/generate.mjs`).
- Explain: ester steps for ring esters; "Los 6 carbonos" count wording fixed for esters only.

## Decisions (from memory)

- `-carboxilato` (P-65.6.3.2.1); `benzoato` retained preferred (P-65.6.3.2); `fenilacetato`
  labelled "acepta", following the `ácido fenilacético` precedent (I-40b).
- Ring diester with different O-bound groups refused (`mixedDiester`: needs per-group locants,
  P-65.6.3.2.2). Ring O-bound groups carry no parentheses (a separate word).
- `esterPrefix` message now says "un oxígeno, un nitrógeno o un anillo".

## Still refused

Two rings with an acid (`ácido 4-(benzoiloxi)…`), a ring ester beside a chain ester
(`esterPrefix`), three esters, polysubstituted benzene, lactones, other heterocycles.

## Review

Codex ship-with-fixes, 1 should-fix: `isLactone()` flagged a cyclic anhydride
(`O=C1OC(=O)CC1`) as a lactone. Fixed by the orchestrator: a lactone now needs a ring carbon
that passes `isEsterCarbon()` with its bridge O in the ring; regression in
`tests/unit/ring-esters.test.js` (anhydrides and a cyclic carbonate get the generic heterocycle
message).

## Open points (pre-existing wording)

- Ring acids, nitriles and amides still say "Los 6 carbonos…" though the group carbon lies
  outside the ring.
- The ring step calls an ester prefix on the ring "una rama".
