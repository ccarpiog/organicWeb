## What was built

- **Tile design (decision):** one tile `oxi` (kind `alkoxy`, value 'ether', text the lexicon's `ALKOXY_ENDING`) in the
  «Grupos» row, written **right after the branch it turns into an alkoxy group** — after `il` (a chain or ring: `met il
  oxi`, `pent il oxi`, `prop 2 il oxi`, `ciclo hex il oxi`), after `isopropil`, `tert-butil` or `fenil`, and for a branch
  with prefixes of its own after its own chain, **before END** (`START 2 met il prop il oxi END`, `START 4 met il fenil oxi
  END`). It is a field (`oxi`) of the chain or retained node, as the forward engine's alkoxy structure is its alkyl
  group's with `alkoxy` set, so every rule that reads a branch reads an alkoxy group's branch too. The **contraction is
  the app's** (design.md §15.5.2 item 6): the student always writes `il`; when render.js would contract (a saturated chain
  of 1–4 carbons by its carbon 1 citing no locant, or a retained word) the `il` tile writes nothing — an empty span, a
  small dashed box in the name line with the accessible name «il (no se escribe)» — and a retained word loses its `il`
  (`isoprop`/`tert-but`/`fen` + `oxi`). Tile order stays text order (one span per tile, in order).
- **Parser:** `simple = stem… (il [oxi] | iliden) | (isopropil | tert-butil | fenil) [oxi] | isopropiliden`. `oxi` after
  `iliden`/`isopropiliden` is TILE_ORDER with its own hint (single bonds only); anywhere else (alone, after a halogen or
  `hidroxi`, after END, after a bare root) TILE_ORDER with a hint saying where it goes. LOCANT_COUNT asks for `bis` before
  an alkoxy prefix the engine multiplies with bis. ENDS_WITH_BRANCH says «un grupo («metoxi»)». `anisol` is a template
  parent like `tolueno` (TEMPLATE_LOCANTS, letters counting its methoxy group, its own branch hint `(4-metoxifenil)`).
- **Assembler (rule 6):** contraction and enclosure as render.js: non-contracted alkoxy prefixes enclosed (`(pentiloxi)`,
  `(eteniloxi)`, `(propan-1-iloxi)` for a cited locant), contracted ones only with prefixes of their own or on a crowded
  level, where an alkoxy prefix is enclosed wherever it stands (`cloro(metoxi)metano`, `(metoxi)yodometano`,
  `difluorodi(metoxi)metano`, `cloro(fenoxi)metano`); nesting levels ( ) then [ ] (`[2-(pentiloxi)etoxi]`); the student's
  multiplier kept, with the hyphen before `tert-` (`bis-tert-butoxi`, `di-tert-butoxi`).
- **Tokenizer:** `oxi` after `il` (OXI); after a root as two tiles, the silent `il` and `oxi` (OXIC); the cut retained words
  before `oxi` (RETX, PHEX); an automatic enclosure may hold `il` + `oxi` or a whole crowded contracted prefix.
- **Interpreter / builder:** SubstituentSpec `alkoxy`; the branch is built as before, then an ether oxygen (last atom of the
  occurrence, so `ownPrefixAtoms()` is unchanged) single-bonded to the branch's free-valence carbon and to the parent's
  carbon; the occurrence's `attachment` is the oxygen. The oxygen counts for the 80-heavy-atom cap
  (`triacontametoxitriacontano` → TOO_BIG), never for the carbon cap or the chains; VALENCE and ranges as for any prefix
  («…: en el anisol, ese carbono ya lleva un grupo metoxi»). Two rings through an ether oxygen are not «bonded atom to
  atom» (`ringOnRing()`): they have a structural key and the engine refuses them itself (`fenoxibenceno` → `outOfScope`,
  «varios anillos»).
- **Search:** nothing new — the prefix's slots are carbon slots of what it hangs from (one bond each), the branch's own
  chain a model chain whose free valence adds one bond (`metoxipropano`: three placements, two structures).
- **Verdicts:** every §15.3 kind for ethers (CASES, MUTATIONS); `etoxietano` is `correct` (its class name `dietil éter`,
  an engine alternative, has no tiles yet); `2-(2-hidroxietoxi)etan-1-ol` → `outOfScope` with the engine's
  `symmetricEther` message (`oxidi-`).
- **Diagnosis (`diagnose.js`, header «Ethers»):** an alkoxy prefix is a hetero group like a halogen: rule 1 skips it
  (`1-metoxi-1-metilpropano` blames `metil`; before, it blamed `metoxi`), the messages say «sustituyentes»
  (MORE_SUBSTITUENTS, UNGROUPED), di/bis follow render.js isCompoundPrefix(), the style shapes carry `O:`. The chain,
  ring and form rules work unchanged otherwise: NUMBERING, RING_NUMBERING (`1-metoxi-4-metilbenceno` at N4: Spanish order
  `metil` < `metoxi`), ALPHABETICAL_ORDER, UNGROUPED, MULTIPLIER_KIND, LOCANT_ORDER, NEEDLESS_LOCANTS (also inside the
  alkoxy branch, `(1-clorometoxi)`, `(propan-1-iloxi)`), MISSING_LOCANTS, OLD_STYLE, SUBSTITUTED_TEMPLATE for `anisol`. The
  parent on the wrong side of the oxygen gets the chain rules' own true messages: LONGER_CHAIN (`etoximetano`, now «1
  carbono» in the singular), MORE_SUBSTITUENTS (`(clorometoxi)metano`), RING_IS_PARENT (`fenoxietano`),
  MORE_HYDROXY_GROUPS (`(2-hidroxietoxi)metano`).
- **Dialog / Ayuda:** the `oxi` tile with its tooltip and the prefix colour, `anisol` with its tooltip; `#help-name-to-drawing`
  now ends «…, y también de sus éteres.» and a new `#help-name-to-drawing-ethers` paragraph explains `oxi` and `anisol`.

## Decisions

- **`oxi` as a closing tile of the branch, not alkoxy words.** Whole-word tiles (`metoxi`, `etoxi`…) would not compose
  with substituted or long groups (`(2-metilpropoxi)`, `(pentiloxi)`) and would let the student skip the contraction rule
  the spec makes automatic; `oxi` after END would put its text inside the marks while its tile follows them (text order ≠
  tile order, which the tokenizer and the name line's selection rely on). `met oxi` (no `il`) is not read: ROOT_END and
  TILE_ORDER tell the student to add `il`, which the app then hides.
- **A cited free-valence locant is never contracted** (`prop 1 il oxi` → `(propan-1-iloxi)`, NEEDLESS_LOCANTS «se escribe
  «1-propoxi»»), as rule 5 reads `(propan-1-il)`: a locant never runs into `oxi`.
- **`MAX_CONTRACTED_ALKOXY` is kept in `assemble.js`** (src/reverse never imports render.js, tested); a test asserts it
  equals render.js's.
- **`bis-tert-butoxi` follows the forward engine** (render.js isCompoundPrefix() counts the tert-butyl structure's own
  methyls for an alkoxy prefix; as an alkyl it stays `di-tert-butil`): the parser asks for `bis`, the diagnosis treats
  it as compound. See open risks.
- **`anisol`** is a TEMPLATES entry (group METHOXY, `alkoxy` set; tile after `mesitileno`); the forward engine gives it
  for the bare metoxibenceno only (aromatic.js traditionalNameId()), so a substituted or lettered one (`4-cloroanisol`,
  `p-cloroanisol`, two positions, accepted by checkLetters()) is `otherName` with SUBSTITUTED_TEMPLATE, as `4-clorotolueno`.
- **Deliberate, tested nulls** (diagnose.js header «Ethers», for I-51b2): `di` before an alkoxy prefix compound only for its
  form (`1,6-di(pentiloxi)hexano`, `1,6-di(propan-2-iloxi)hexano`, `1,6-di-tert-butoxihexano`): MULTIPLIER_KIND's reason
  «una rama que tiene sus propias ramas» does not hold; and, as for any branch, an alkoxy group in a prefix style the
  engine does not offer for the molecule. No sweep produces a null (fixture and generated sweeps assert none).

## Deviations / open risks

- None from the brief's requirements. Beyond them: an ether tokenize property test, the `anisol` branch hint, the silent
  piece's accessible name, the LONGER_CHAIN singular (it only arises with ethers: a one-carbon student chain).
- **Open question (forward engine, not changed):** render.js writes `1,6-bis-tert-butoxihexano`; IUPAC usage is
  `di-tert-butoxy` (tert-butoxy is a simple prefix). The reverse engine follows the engine, so the IUPAC-style
  `1,6-di-tert-butoxihexano` is `otherName` with a null diagnosis. A fix belongs to `src/naming/render.js` (user's call).
- The wrong-side parent (`etoximetano`) is diagnosed with chain-rule messages that do not mention the ether oxygen;
  ether-specific wording is I-51b2's. Class names (`dietil éter`) cannot be built yet; the Ayuda says ethers are read
  («y también de sus éteres»), which holds for the substitutive names.
- PROGRESS.md *Open risks* «`cloro(metoxi)metano` brackets → R-5b» is now closed (orchestrator's file, not edited here).
