## What was built

- **Tiles:** «Grupos» gains `oxo` (kind `carbonyl`, value `ketone`, the lexicon's `GROUP_PREFIXES.ketone`), `formil` (kind
  `retained`, value `formyl`, `FORMYL_PREFIX`), `al`, `ona` (kind `suffix`, `GROUP_SUFFIXES`) and `carbaldehído` (kind `suffix`,
  value `carbaldehyde`, `RING_GROUP_SUFFIXES.aldehyde`), after `éter`; «Anillos» gains `benzaldehído` (kind `benzene`, value
  `benzaldehyde`, `BENZENE_GROUP_NAMES.aldehyde`) after `fenol`. `SUFFIX_GROUPS` gives each suffix tile its group (`kind` as the
  engine's SuffixStructure, `outside`, the C–O bond `order`, `terminal` for `al`); `GROUP_WORD_PARENTS` the group `fenol` and
  `benzaldehído` place at carbon 1.
- **Grammar:** `suffix = [locants] [MULT] (ol | al | ona | carbaldehído)`, `group = … | oxo`, `simple = … | formil`, `parent = … |
  fenol | benzaldehído`. `al` on a chain takes no locants (TILE_ORDER over its own or the old-style ones that could only be its:
  `butan-1-al`, `butan-2-al`, `1-butanal`), and has no list to leave open, so `2-butenal` gives its locant to `eno`; `al` after a
  `ciclo` ring or `benceno` is TILE_ORDER (their –CHO is `carbaldehído`; `bencenal`); `ona`/`carbaldehído` take locants, old style
  (`2-pentanona`) or none. `bis` before a suffix and a stray suffix have hints per suffix. `benzaldehído` is `fenol`'s twin: no
  suffix, numbers or letter of its own (TEMPLATE_LOCANTS «…su grupo CHO, en el carbono 1…»), counted by an o/m/p letter
  (`p-clorobenzaldehído`). `formil oxi` and `formil` in an ether's class name are TILE_ORDER with their hints.
- **Assembler:** rule 4 already decided the elision by the first letter of multiplier + suffix, as render.js suffixWords(): `butanal`,
  `but-2-enal`, `prop-2-inal`, `pentan-2-ona`, `ciclohexanona`, `ciclohex-2-en-1-ona`, `hexano-2,3,4,5-tetrona`, but `butanodial`,
  `but-2-enodial`, `pentano-2,4-diona`, `ciclohexanocarbaldehído`, `ciclohex-3-eno-1-carbaldehído`, `benceno-1,4-dicarbaldehído`.
  Forty forms are checked against the engine's own text and tokenized back.
- **Interpreter / builder:** SuffixSpec `{kind, outside, slots, multiplier, word, phenol, placed, terminal, tiles}`. `al` on a chain:
  implicit slots at carbon 1 and, with `dial`, at the last carbon, on the `al` tiles. `ona`, `carbaldehído`: carbon slots 1…n.
  `benzaldehído`: one implicit slot at 1, `placed`, `outside`. Build puts a double-bonded oxygen on the parent's carbon for `al` and
  `ona`, and for `carbaldehído`/`benzaldehído` a new carbon single-bonded to it with the =O on that carbon (`suffixAtoms` entries get
  `carbon` and `carbonBond`; the carbon counts for both caps, both atoms for the heavy-atom cap). `oxo` is a one-atom group bonded by
  a double bond (`atomGroupOrder()`), `formil` a retained template (one carbon with its =O). New structural code
  **CARBONYL_POSITION**: more –CHO groups of `al` than the chain has ends (`butanotrial`, `metanodial`; checkSpec()), and a ketone's
  C=O on a carbon bonded to fewer than two carbons once built (`propan-1-ona`, `metanona`, `1-cloropropan-1-ona`; an end carbon a
  ring or a branch makes a ketone's is right: `1-feniletan-1-ona`, `1-metilpropan-1-ona`). VALENCE on an end carbon `al` takes by
  rule blames the student's locant and says why («…: el grupo CHO de «al» va en ese carbono», `1,1,1-tricloroetanal`); on
  `benzaldehído`'s carbon 1, «…en el benzaldehído, ese carbono ya lleva un grupo CHO».
- **Search:** no new role. A suffix's C=O takes two bonds of its carbon, a `carbaldehído`'s –CHO one, `oxo` two, `formil` one; a
  ketone placement on a bare end is left to build() (CARBONYL_POSITION), like TOO_BIG.
- **Diagnosis (`diagnose.js`, header «Aldehydes and ketones»):** the rules run for a carbonyl name where they say something true —
  BRANCH_AT_END, LONGER_CHAIN and P2–P4 after a P0 tie, NUMBERING/RING_NUMBERING at N1–N4 after an N0 tie, ALPHABETICAL_ORDER (`oxo`
  under o), UNGROUPED, MULTIPLIER_KIND, LOCANT_ORDER (the suffix's too), MISSING_LOCANTS, OLD_STYLE with the suffix's words
  (`suffixWordOf()`: «…justo delante de «ona»», «diona»); `oxo` is a hetero group like a halogen («sustituyentes», never lengthens
  a chain), shape `X:=O`; `formil` the engine's formyl shape. Deliberate nulls below.
- **Dialog:** the six tiles with tooltips; `oxo` in the prefix colour (`.nb-tile--carbonyl`).

## Decisions

- **`ona` keeps the carbon role (1…n) and the builder judges the end carbon.** design.md §15.5.3 sketches an inner-carbon role,
  but the engine's own `1-feniletan-1-ona` / `1-ciclohexiletan-1-ona` put the ketone on an end carbon that a ring makes a ketone's;
  an inner-only domain would leave `feniletanona` with no placement. A C=O of `ona` on a carbon bonded to fewer than two carbons is
  CARBONYL_POSITION with the design's message, widened to say why («…va en un carbono unido a otros dos carbonos, como los del
  interior de la cadena; en un extremo sería un aldehído («al»).»).
- **`al` takes no locants at all** (design.md §15.5.3: implicit by rule): a locant on it, whether the end or an inner carbon, is one
  TILE_ORDER hint saying the –CHO is always at an end, rather than `butan-1-al` → otherName and `butan-2-al` → invalid; the old-style
  locants of `2-butenal` go to `eno`.
- **`carbaldehído` after a chain is read** (`butano-1-carbaldehído` → otherName «pentanal», `butanocarbaldehído` → ambiguous): IUPAC
  has the construction (`propano-1,2,3-tricarbaldehído`, which the engine refuses: outOfScope), so the name describes a molecule;
  its diagnosis is a deliberate null.
- **`oxo` is a one-atom group (kind `carbonyl`) with a double bond**, sharing every code path of the halogens and `hidroxi`
  (`isAtomGroup()`), its bond order from `atomGroupOrder()`. The kind is not `oxo` because a kind string equal to a tile text breaks
  the «never retypes a tile word» rule.
- **`formil` is a retained word** (a fixed one-carbon group with its =O): every branch path (enclosure, prefixes refused inside a
  compound branch, BRANCH_AT_END) works unchanged.
- **`benzaldehído` is one whole-word tile** like `fenol` (the engine's main name), in «Anillos»; `benceno` + `carbaldehído` reads the
  systematic forms (`bencenocarbaldehído`, `4-clorobenceno-1-carbaldehído`, `benceno-1,4-dicarbaldehído`).
- **Deliberate, tested nulls** (diagnose.js header «Aldehydes and ketones», for I-51c3): before any rule, the student's suffix is not
  the engine's (the principal C=O written `oxo` or `formil`: `2-oxopropano`, `formilciclohexano`, `(4-formilfenil)metano`; `ona`
  beside the engine's aldehyde, `5-oxopentan-2-ona`; `ol` beside a ketone, `4-oxopentan-2-ol`; `al` on a carbon a branch makes a
  ketone's, `1-metilpropanal`; another number of groups, `5-oxopentanal`; `carbaldehído` on a chain), or the parent's kind differs
  (`1-(2-oxociclohexil)propan-2-ona`); a chain or numbering dropped at P0 or N0 (`pentan-4-ona`, `ciclohexan-3-ona`,
  `hexano-3,5-diona`: their messages speak of OH groups); the suffix's needless locants (`ciclohexan-1-ona`,
  `ciclohexano-1-carbaldehído`). An OH on an aldehyde's carbon builds an acid (`1-hidroxipropanal` → «ácido propanoico»): null, a later
  family's group, as before.

  join it, and so do 2 more carbonyl-ether rows of other sections the ether filter had never seen (I-39b `2-(2-formilbutoxi)butanodial`,
  I-43b `4-metoxibenzaldehído` with 2 alternatives): 7 rows, 9 names. The 2 acid rows (`ácido 2-metoxietanoico`,
  `ácido 2-metoxipropanoico`) stay asserted left out for R-5d.
- The Ayuda still says the app reads hydrocarbons, halogen derivatives, alcohols, phenols and ethers: the aldehyde/ketone line is
  I-51c3's, with the messages.
- Many mutation `otherName` verdicts get a null diagnosis this phase (fixture sweep: 118 of 168, mostly N0 renumberings): expected
  until I-51c3 words the carbonyl messages; the sweeps classify every null by its reason and fail on any other.
- `1-cloropropan-1-ona` (an acyl chloride) gets the ketone message («…en un extremo sería un aldehído»), true of the bare end only.
- design.md §15.8's Status column is not updated (the user's uncommitted file).
