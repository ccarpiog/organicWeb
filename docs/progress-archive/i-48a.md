## Decisions

- A "start of name" insertion point (Home or a click) so a tile can go in front; «Borrar ficha»
  does nothing while it is selected (like Backspace at the start of a text).
- Tile cap 100; at the cap tile buttons get `aria-disabled` (focus is kept) and a short message.
- New Spanish drafts pending teacher review: the row labels, the empty-name placeholder,
  «Principio del nombre», «menos», the cap message.
