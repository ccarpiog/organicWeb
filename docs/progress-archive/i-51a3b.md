## What was built

- **HYDROXY_PREFIX** (rule 1, step `group`, checked before every rule): a parent-level `hidroxi` whose oxygen is one of
  the engine's suffix oxygens (SuffixLocant `attachAtom`) — an OH the engine cites as `-ol`. «El grupo –OH es el grupo
  principal: se nombra con la terminación «-ol», al final del nombre, no con el prefijo «hidroxi». Se escribe
  «propan-2-ol».»; with several OH groups «…los de la cadena principal / del anillo se nombran todos juntos con la
  terminación «-diol»…». A `hidroxi` the engine writes too (inside a compound branch, or on a chain or ring that is not
  the parent) never fires it. On `4-hidroxitolueno` and the retained phenols it replaces SUBSTITUTED_TEMPLATE, which
  would call the OH a «rama».
- **HYDROXY_PARENT** (rule 1, in place of RING_IS_PARENT): the student's parent is a chain and the engine's the ring, or
  the reverse, in a molecule with OH groups. The counts come from the engine's `RINGCHAIN` step (without it every OH is on
  the ring): «La cadena principal es la que lleva más grupos –OH, sea el anillo o una cadena abierta; solo si empatan
  manda el anillo. Aquí el anillo y la mejor cadena llevan uno cada uno, así que gana el anillo: «…».» Step `ringChain`
  («Anillo o cadena»), or `benzene` / `ring` when the trace has no `RINGCHAIN` step; trace rule `RINGCHAIN` / `RING`.
- **MORE_HYDROXY_GROUPS** (rule 2, P0, step `groupChain`): the student's chain dropped at P0. `chainRules()` now tests
  leaf-to-leaf against the first chain step (P0 when present, else P1, unchanged for hydrocarbons). A chain that is no
  leaf-to-leaf path and that no chain P0 keeps contains (dropped at P0 however lengthened) is MORE_HYDROXY_GROUPS before
  BRANCH_AT_END (`fewerHydroxyGroups()`, review fix below).
- **N0**: `numberingReasons.N0` (the explanation's own wording of N0) and `lowerLocantsOf.N0`, so NUMBERING,
  RING_NUMBERING and CHAIN_LOCANTS (DROPPED_AT N0) cover the OH numbering; the guard that nulled N0 is gone.
- **PHENOL** (rule 2, step `group`, in ringRules() after the template check): `benceno` + one `ol` where the engine names
  the molecule from `fenol`.
- **Form rules:** NEEDLESS_LOCANTS reads the suffix's supplied slots (omitted with the prefixes', parentOmissions());
  OLD_STYLE names the suffix's words when the old-style locants are the suffix's (`tree.parent.legacySuffix`).
- **outsideRules():** null only for a principal group other than the OH (a later family's; no tile writes one today) or
  the impossible hydrocarbon case kept from before.

## Decisions

- **New codes where no existing one fits, variants where one does.** N0 is a variant of NUMBERING / RING_NUMBERING /
  CHAIN_LOCANTS (same rule, another trace step), the suffix's needless and old-style locants variants of NEEDLESS_LOCANTS
  and OLD_STYLE (so draw-name.js keeps dropping the old-style note beside OLD_STYLE with no change). An OH written
  `hidroxi`, the OH-decided parent, P0 and `bencenol` say things no existing message says: four codes.
- **Order:** the explanation names the principal group before it looks for the parent, so HYDROXY_PREFIX runs first,
  then HYDROXY_PARENT, then the chain or ring rules. HYDROXY_PREFIX and HYDROXY_PARENT exclude each other (a `hidroxi` on
  the student's chain parent cannot be on the engine's ring, and the reverse).
- **`(4-hidroxifenil)metano`** (listed with the OH-count cases) is HYDROXY_PARENT: the OH is in the student's phenyl
  branch, not on the student's parent, and what decides the parent is that the ring carries it.
- **CHAIN_LOCANTS at N0 does occur** when chains as long carry different sets of OH groups (three arms around one
  carbon: `3-(2-hidroxietil)pentano-2,4-diol`), so it has its phrase and a case.
- **No deliberate null left** for the listed cases; the fixture mutation sweep has no null family any more.

## Review

Codex adversarial review (`docs/reviews/I-51a3b.md`): ship-with-fixes, 0 blockers, 1 should-fix.

### Review resolution

- **Should-fix (`diagnose.js` `chainRules()`): an end-branch diagnosis contradicted OH priority.**
  `1-metil-2-(hidroximetil)hexan-1-ol` (→ `2-butilbutano-1,3-diol`) was BRANCH_AT_END («…la cadena principal tiene 4
  carbonos»), while the step it opens says the chain with the most OH groups wins. Cause: a student's chain that is no
  leaf-to-leaf path went straight to BRANCH_AT_END, before P0 was read. Fix: `fewerHydroxyGroups()` runs first there. When
  no chain P0 keeps contains the student's chain (P0 drops it however lengthened), the diagnosis is MORE_HYDROXY_GROUPS (P0,
  step `groupChain`), with the student's count its chain's own as P0 counts it (`substituent.js` `suffixSites()`).
  Otherwise BRANCH_AT_END stays. The review suggested comparing the chain's own OH count. The containment test was chosen
  instead because the own-count test would also blame `1-(hidroximetil)propan-1-ol` (→ `butano-1,2-diol`, the molecule's
  one chain), whose step says «Todos los carbonos forman una sola cadena», which «Hay otra cadena con más grupos –OH» would
  contradict. Hydrocarbons and halogen derivatives (no P0) take the old path unchanged.
- **Sweep for the same precedence bug:** no other rule runs ahead of P0 on a chain P0 drops, except HYDROXY_PREFIX. That
  rule states that the OH is the principal group and quotes the engine's name, so it never contradicts OH priority, and
  its first place is the phase's decision (Decisions, «Order»): left as is. LONGER_CHAIN and the trace rules follow P0 in
  the trace, and the form rules run only after the chain rules. An ad-hoc sweep of 135,590 generated alcohol names (chains
  of 1–6 carbons, 0–2 OH groups, one or two branches among alkyl, hydroxyalkyl, `hidroxi`, `cloro`, `ciclohexil`, `fenil`
  and hydroxy rings) found no chain P0 drops with a non-OH diagnosis, and every MORE_HYDROXY_GROUPS opens a step that
  states the OH-count rule.
- **Tests:** a new test in `reverse-diagnose-alcohols.test.js` covers the review's case and three others: a different end
  branch, 0 against 1 OH, and a cyclohexyl ring with a `RINGCHAIN` step. Each is checked for its code, message and the
  step text. The controls keep BRANCH_AT_END: as many OH groups, the missing OH in the end branch, a hydrocarbon and a
  halogen derivative.
