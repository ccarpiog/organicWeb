# I-40c — Ring nitriles and amides (split from I-40)

- Risk: high. Worker: opus. Third of the four I-40 splits (I-40a ring prefixes, I-40b ring acids and
  aldehydes, this phase, I-40d ring esters); design §13.4 row I-40c.

## What changed

- `principal.js`: `RING_GROUP_KINDS` and `isRingGroupCarbon()` now take a principal amide or –C≡N carbon
  bonded to a ring atom; everything else of I-40b (ring-or-chain count, X out of chain skeletons,
  `suffixSites(…, ring = true)`, `outside` suffix, N0 numbering, rendering) follows unchanged.
  `carbonylTraditionalId()`: `phenylacetamide`, `phenylacetonitrile`; a phenyl on the amide N is not the
  C-phenyl case (it gives `acetamide`: `N-fenilacetamida`).
- `substituent.js nitrogenSubstituents()`: on a ring parent it also reads the N on the carbon X outside the
  ring (`N-metilbenzamida`).
- `lexicon.es.js` / `lexicon.en.js`: `carboxamida` / `carbonitrilo` suffixes; `benz` + `amida`,
  `benz` + `onitrilo`; traditional `2-fenilacetamida`, `fenilacetonitrilo`.
- `aromatic.js traditionalNameId()`: no traditional name for a benzene with a suffix other than the amine
  (`N-metilbenzamida` was offered «tolueno» for its N-methyl).
- `index.js carbonylAlternative()`: the N-prefix rendering only for `formamide` / `acetamide`.
- `validate.js`: `amidePlacementError()` / `nitrilePlacementError()` lose the ring refusals and count per
  `chainPieces()` (ring atoms left out); `substitutedPolyamide` also for two or more amides bonded to a ring
  that is the parent (ring count ≥ the best piece) with a group on some N.
- `explain.js`: `OUTSIDE_GROUP_WORDS` / `RING_GROUP_NAMES` for amide and nitrile; `ringGroupSentences()`
  generalised; amide and nitrile group steps use it (N groups off the ring; a –C≡N on a branch of the ring);
  ring step (O / N not in the ring); benzene step and assemble use the retained word (`benzamida` in
  `N-metilbenzamida`) and mention the N groups; assemble no longer says «su carbono siempre es el 1» for an
  outside group; `cyanoCarbonSentence()`, `amidePrefixSentences()` and `carbamoylDescription()` say «anillo»
  on a ring parent; `traditionalSentence()` says «acepta» for names IUPAC only accepts.
- Oracle: `generateRingNitrilesAmides()` (ring, side chain, both, a ring on the N, prefix forms below an
  acid), `ringGroupGraft()` takes `amide` / `nitrile`, `addRandomRing()` factored out of `ringAcylGraft()`
  (same random sequence), wired into `run.mjs`.

## Decisions (IUPAC 2013; from memory where marked)

- `-carboxamida` (P-66.1.1.4) and `-carbonitrilo` (P-66.5.1.1) after the full ring name, the ring atom
  bonded to X locant 1, omitted as a lone group on a saturated ring (from memory; same rule as
  `-carboxílico`).
- `benzamida` and `benzonitrilo` are retained preferred names (P-66.1.1.1.2, P-66.5.1.1.2, from memory);
  `bencenocarboxamida` / `bencenocarbonitrilo` offered as `benzeneSystematic` with the I-40b label.
- `N-feniletanamida` is the main name (the app's systematic-first policy), `N-fenilacetamida` the
  traditional one with the existing `acetamide` label («conserva como preferido»: acetamide is a retained
  preferred name, from memory); `acetanilida` not offered.
- `2-fenilacetamida` keeps its locant 2 (the N could carry the phenyl too) and `fenilacetonitrilo` has none;
  both labelled «acepta», as `ácido fenilacético` (status from memory).
- An amide bonded to a ring through its N below an acid stays `acilamino-` (`(acetilamino)`, `(benzoilamino)`),
  not IUPAC's `acetamido` / `benzamido` (I-39d decision kept); a phenyl on the N of an `amino…oxo` prefix
  is `(fenilamino)`, not `anilino` (I-40a decision kept).
- Two or more amides on a ring parent with a group on some N are refused (`substitutedPolyamide`, N¹/N²
  locants), like a chain diamide.

## Refusals

- Lifted: `ringNitrile`, `ringAmide` (messages removed). Widened: `substitutedPolyamide` (ring parent).
  Narrowed: `manyAmides`, `manyNitriles` (ring atoms out of the pieces). Unchanged: `ringEster` (I-40d),
  `carbonocyanidic`, `polysubstitutedBenzene`. No new refusal reason.
