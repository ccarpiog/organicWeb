## What was built

- **Tiles:** «Grupos» gains, at its end, `ciano` (a retained word), `nitrilo` and `carbonitrilo` (suffixes); «Anillos» gains
  `benzonitrilo` after `benzoato`. Every text from the lexicon. SUFFIX_GROUPS `nitrile` (terminal, like `oico`) and `carbonitrile`
  (outside, like `carboxílico`), both `order` 3 and `element` 'N' (suffixElement(): the group's atom, an oxygen for every other
  suffix); GROUP_WORD_PARENTS `benzonitrile` (its –C≡N at carbon 1, «grupo C≡N»).
- **Grammar:** `suffix = … | nitrilo | carbonitrilo`; `parent = … | benzonitrilo`; `simple = … | ciano`. `nitrilo` mirrors `oico`:
  no locants (TILE_ORDER, also old-style ones that could only be its: `1-butanonitrilo`), old style goes to the segment
  (`2-butenonitrilo`), TILE_ORDER after a ring or `benceno` with its own hints. `carbonitrilo` takes locants like `carboxílico`, and
  after a chain it is read (the engine judges it). `benzonitrilo` is `benzoico`'s twin (TEMPLATE_LOCANTS, counted by an o/m/p
  letter: `p-metilbenzonitrilo`). `ciano` is `carboxi`'s twin (`oxi` after it, in an ether's class name or as an O-bound group:
  TILE_ORDER with hints).
- **Assembler / tokenizer:** nothing of their own: the suffixes start with a consonant, so rules 3 and 4 keep the parent's `o`
  (`butanonitrilo`, `prop-2-enonitrilo`, `butanodinitrilo`, `ciclohex-2-eno-1-carbonitrilo`), as render.js does; `ciano` is never
  enclosed by itself (`diciano`, `(cianometil)`, `cianometilo`, `bis(cianometilo)`); the tokenizer reads the suffixes as OL,
  `benzonitrilo` as PHOL, `ciano` as RET. 52 forms are checked against the engine's own text and tokenized back.
- **Interpreter / builder:** SuffixSpec `kind` 'nitrile' (no new field). Build puts a nitrogen triple-bonded to the –C≡N's carbon:
  the chain end for `nitrilo` (three bonds of it; implicit slots at 1 and, with `dinitrilo`, the last carbon), a new carbon outside
  for `carbonitrilo` and `benzonitrilo`. `ciano` is a retained template: one carbon with its N, bonded by that carbon (never a carbon
  of the chain). The caps count the –C≡N's carbon and its N. CARBONYL_POSITION (reused) for more –C≡N groups of `nitrilo` than the
  chain has ends; VALENCE on a `nitrilo` end names «nitrilo»; on `benzonitrilo`'s carbon 1, «…en el benzonitrilo, ese carbono ya
  lleva un grupo C≡N».
- **Search:** no new role; suffixBondAmount(): three bonds for `nitrilo`, one for `carbonitrilo`, `ciano` one.
- **Diagnosis (`diagnose.js`, header «Nitriles»):** outsideRules() lets the nitriles through; nitrileChecks() runs first in
  firstRule() and answers BEYOND (the reading's null) for the nitrile's own mistakes; BEYOND guards in chainDropped() (P0, N0),
  fewerPrincipalGroups(), directionDraft() (N0), principalParent(), needlessLocants() and missingLocants() (the suffix's locants),
  firstRule() turning a parent rule's BEYOND into null. Every other rule runs and was checked to say something true of a nitrile
  name: BRANCH_AT_END, LONGER_CHAIN, MORE_SUBSTITUENTS, MORE_MULTIPLE_BONDS, CHAIN_LOCANTS (N1–N4), NUMBERING, RING_NUMBERING
  (N1–N4), BENZENE_RING, BRANCH_RING_NUMBERING, ACYL_NAME, ALPHABETICAL_ORDER (`ciano` under c: «ciano» va antes que «cloro» (i
  antes que l)), UNGROUPED, MULTIPLIER_KIND, LOCANT_ORDER, NEEDLESS_LOCANTS inside a branch, OLD_STYLE («eno» o «ino»). `ciano` has
  the engine's shape for the prefix style (`X:CN`), is a hetero group for rule 1 and a «sustituyente» for P4.
- **Dialog:** the four tiles with tooltips.

## Decisions

- **`ciano` is a retained word (kind 'retained', value 'cyano'), `carboxi`'s twin**, not a one-atom group: it is two atoms, and the
  forward engine's cyano prefix is a simple prefix like `carboxi` (never enclosed, `diciano`). RETAINED_TEMPLATES gains a `nitrile`
  field (carbons carrying a triple-bonded N), every other template's empty.
- **The suffix's atom by element (SUFFIX_GROUPS `element`, suffixElement())** rather than a nitrile flag on the SuffixSpec: build.js
  attachSuffix() creates the group's atom with that element and the entry's bond order (3); nothing else needed to know it is a
  nitrogen (the search counts bonds through suffixBondAmount(), the verdict's `suffixAtoms` keep their shape: `attachment` is the N).
- **`carbonitrilo` after a chain is read and judged, not a parse error**, following the `carbaldehído` / `carboxílico` /
  `carboxilato` precedent the brief asks to follow (the engine never writes it there, but the student's reading has one molecule):
  `propano-1-carbonitrilo` → `otherName` «butanonitrilo», a «Nitriles» `carbon` null for I-52b3, as `ácido propano-1-carboxílico`
  became ACID_CARBON.
- **No new error code; CARBONYL_POSITION reused** for the –C≡N groups of `nitrilo` beyond the chain's ends, worded for them, as the
  acids and esters reused it.
- **`ciano` is a hetero group for rule 1** (diagnose.js isHeteroGroup()): the engine never counts its carbon in a chain (IUPAC 2013
  P-66.5), so a `ciano` on an end never lengthens it, and BRANCH_AT_END must never blame it; for P4 it is a «sustituyente», and the
  parser's ENDS_WITH_BRANCH calls it «un grupo», as a halogen.
- **nitrileChecks() comes first, before the ester's and acid's checks**: a student's nitrile suffix beside a senior group
  (`3-carboxipropanonitrilo` → `ácido 3-cianopropanoico`) would otherwise get the acid's CARBONYL_SENIORITY, whose words put the
  junior group's carbon in the chain — false for a `ciano`.
- **Sweeps apart:** the nitrile esters and acyl nitriles read in their families' round trips, but their names are swept in the
  nitrile file, so every earlier family's sweep keeps its names and exact counts (I-52a2b's precedent for the ester prefixes).
- **The messages call the group «grupo C≡N»** (NITRILE_GROUP), the parser's and builder's convention for «grupo COOH» / «grupo COO»
  (the diagnosis's phrases, with the dash, are I-52b3's).
- **Chemistry:** nothing from memory; every name, form and refusal is the forward engine's.

## Deliberate nulls («Nitriles», for I-52b3)

- **Categories** (read independently in the tests, `nitrileCategory()`): `seniority` — the student's suffix cites no –C≡N while the
  engine's principal group is one (`cianometano` → «etanonitrilo», `1-cianopropano`, `cianobenceno`, `4-cianobutan-2-ona` →
  «4-oxopentanonitrilo»), or cites one beside a senior group (`3-carboxipropanonitrilo` → «ácido 3-cianopropanoico»); `count` —
  another number of –C≡N groups (`4-cianobutanonitrilo` → «pentanodinitrilo», `3-ciano-2-cloropropanonitrilo`); `carbon` — the
  –C≡N's carbon on the other side of the parent (`propano-1-carbonitrilo` → «butanonitrilo», `ciclohexilmetanonitrilo` →
  «ciclohexanocarbonitrilo», `fenilmetanonitrilo` → «benzonitrilo»); `numbering` — the –C≡N groups numbered otherwise (N0:
  `ciclohexano-1,5-dicarbonitrilo`, `benceno-1,5-dicarbonitrilo`); `suffix` — the suffix's needless locants
  (`ciclohexano-1-carbonitrilo`, `benceno-1-carbonitrilo`). Guarded too, though no case was found: the parent chosen by the –C≡N
  groups (P0, `RINGCHAIN`; `parent` in the tests), CHAIN_LOCANTS at N0, the suffix's missing locant.
- **A reading of a name with the suffix's locant left out:** diagnose() tries every placement of the one molecule as a reading and
  keeps null when one reading gets null; a placement that numbers the –C≡N otherwise is an N0 null, so `5,2-dimetilbencenocarbonitrilo`
  and `3,6-dimetoxibencenocarbonitrilo` get no diagnosis, where the acid's analogues get LOCANT_ORDER and RING_NUMBERING (tested). It
  goes with the N0 null; the acids had the same until I-51d3.
- **Counts:** deliberate-null test 14 cases (seniority 5, count 2, carbon 3, numbering 2, suffix 2) and the two readings above;
  fixture sweep 72 of 122 `otherName` (all `numbering`); generated sweep 283 of 1149 (all `numbering`); no unexpected null.
